## World-level folders
- `World_Panorama`: definitive wide establishing view of the whole world.
- `World_Map`: top-down layout, boundaries, routes and elevation.
- `World_Moodboard`: palette, materials, lighting and shape language.
- `Landmarks`: signature landmark sheets and close-ups.
- `Ecosystem`: habitats, plant communities and ambient organisms.
- `Weather_Atmosphere`: daylight, night, weather and rare conditions.
- `Events`: temporary transformations and major world moments.
- `Materials_Props`: terrain, rocks, water, vegetation, structures and props.

Every region contains eight image categories: `01_Hero_Views`, `02_Alternate_Views`, `03_Aerial_and_Topdown`, `04_Landmarks`, `05_Flora_and_Fauna`, `06_Environment_Details`, `07_Lighting_and_Atmosphere`, `08_Secrets_and_Discoveries`. Aim for a hero view, alternate angles, aerial/top-down layout, landmark views, local flora/fauna, close-up details, lighting/weather variations and secret discovery views.

**Style:** VIREL stylized realism. Believable natural forms, distinctive botanical silhouettes, rich controlled colors, cinematic natural lighting and detailed environments. No text, logos or UI in environment images unless specifically requested.

**Naming:** `pelagora_Region_Subject_View_V01.png`. Preserve originals; use new version suffixes for edits.
