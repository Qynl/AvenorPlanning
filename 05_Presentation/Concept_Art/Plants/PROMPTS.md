# Plant Image Prompts

Copy-paste prompts for generating the missing plant art. Save each result as `Plants/<plant-slug>/<plant-slug>_Hero_V01.png`.

**Global style** — VIREL stylized realism. Believable natural forms, distinctive botanical silhouettes, rich controlled colors, cinematic natural light. No text, no logos, no UI, no watermark.

**Every prompt assumes:** one clear specimen, complete silhouette visible, eye-level hero view, shallow environmental context rather than a full scene, readable at thumbnail size.

**Extra views** — reuse the same prompt and swap the closing shot line for one of these:

- *Alternate view:* `Three-quarter view from slightly above, same specimen and lighting.`
- *Silhouette:* `Flat black silhouette on a pale neutral background, profile view, no interior detail.`
- *Detail:* `Macro close-up of the defining structure, shallow depth of field, environment falling away.`
- *Scale:* `Specimen beside a plain 1.8 m human reference figure for scale, muted background.`
- *Growth:* `Four-stage growth sheet, seed to mature specimen, evenly spaced on a neutral background.`
