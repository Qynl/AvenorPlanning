# Prompt Pack

Ready-to-use AI image prompts for the art that is still missing. Generate, then save each image at the path given in its prompt so it drops straight into the library.

**Global style** — VIREL stylized realism. Believable natural forms, distinctive botanical silhouettes, rich controlled colors, cinematic natural lighting, detailed environments. No text, logos, UI or watermarks. Save at 3:2 or 16:9, at least 1536 px on the long edge, JPEG or PNG.

**Pack files**

- [Plants](Plants/PROMPTS.md) — 50 species, one hero prompt each, plus alternate, silhouette, detail, scale and growth variants.
- [Maps](11_Maps_and_Diagrams/PROMPTS.md) — 10 world maps and a per-region top-down formula covering all 60 regions.
- [Thornrunner](03_Creatures_and_Encounters/Thornrunner/PROMPTS.md) — the last creature without a sheet.

**Do not regenerate what exists.** 78 images are already filed; check the [library index](README.md) and the world READMEs before making more.
