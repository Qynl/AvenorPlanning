# Cinematic System

## Camera language
Third-person is the normal gameplay perspective. Cinematic camera framing is used for important inspections, discoveries, world arrivals and ability-set unlocks.

## Cinematic categories
- Micro-cinematics for routine scans: short and skippable.
- Discovery reveals for new species.
- Major reveals for hidden regions and significant discoveries.
- World arrival sequences.
- Ability unlock sequences.
- Story and live-event scenes.

## Multiplayer rules
- Do not trap nearby players in long unwanted sequences.
- Prefer local presentation for personal discoveries.
- Shared world events may use synchronised sequences only when the gameplay context supports it.
- Provide skip/accessibility options where appropriate.

## Production rule
Cinematics must be designed around reusable camera, animation and audio systems rather than one-off scripts for every plant.
