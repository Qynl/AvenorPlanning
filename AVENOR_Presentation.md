# AVENOR — Player Presentation & Profile Cosmetics

> This document defines character identity and player presentation across the home screen, Open World, competitive playlists and social spaces. Existing cosmetic catalogues remain valid concept libraries subject to these shared rules.

## 1. Design direction

AVENOR presentation should feel personal, premium and recognisable. It should reward achievement and express player identity without interfering with movement, combat or discovery.

Shared rules:
- Use the universal rarity hierarchy: Commonplace, Altered, Evolved, Ascendant, Extraordinary, Singular.
- Rarity controls visual/audio complexity and collectability, never gameplay power.
- Names, objective state, combat information and interactions remain readable.
- All effects have volume controls and reduced-effects options where practical.
- Players can mute or reduce other players' cosmetic presentation.
- No cosmetic grants hidden hitbox, visibility, movement or information advantages.

## 2. Character presentation

- Third-person character showcase in the home screen.
- Character customisation is separate from weapon loadout.
- Outfits, accessories, banners and titles are cosmetic.
- First-person gameplay should not show cosmetic geometry that blocks the view.
- Character cosmetics must fit the modern stylised-realistic setting.
- Avoid pay-to-win camouflage or effects that make players harder to identify.

## 3. Home screen showcase

- Display equipped character and primary weapon.
- Support inspection and restrained idle animation.
- Show current world artwork and event theme in the background.
- Allow players to preview owned cosmetics.
- Keep interface contrast and navigation readable.

## 4. Match presentation

MVP and post-match presentation are recognition moments, not extra win conditions.
- Use mode-aware contribution signals.
- Avoid always selecting only the player with the most eliminations.
- Keep team and player results readable.
- Let players skip or reduce long presentation sequences.
- Effects must not delay rematch or queue flow unnecessarily.

## 5. Player banners and profile

Profile may show:
- Character portrait.
- Banner and title.
- Account level.
- Competitive rank.
- Favourite or most-used weapon.
- Weapon mastery highlights.
- Discovery achievements.
- Selected event/archive achievements.

Privacy and visibility settings must be considered before showing detailed statistics.

## 6. Seasonal presentation

Seasonal events may offer:
- Character outfits.
- Weapon skins.
- Emotes.
- Banners and titles.
- Kill and win effects.
- Menu showcase themes.

Seasonal rarity uses the same six-tier hierarchy. Event cosmetics should have clearly stated acquisition conditions and should not require excessive daily play.

## 7. Ownership and equip rules

- Cosmetic ownership is permanent.
- World rotation never removes cosmetics.
- Competitive matches do not grant weapon unlocks, but may grant cosmetic rewards.
- Equip slots and conflict rules must be consistent across modes.
- Cosmetic previews should clearly show whether an item is owned, available or event-limited.
