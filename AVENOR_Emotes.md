> **Current AVENOR integration rules (2026 direction):** Emotes are permanent cosmetic ownership and remain available through every world rotation. They can be used in the home screen and suitable social/safe spaces. During Open World or competitive gameplay they must be interruptible and must never grant invulnerability, hide a character, alter collision or create a combat advantage. Seasonal events may add emotes, but event rewards need a fair return path. The existing six-tier rarity hierarchy remains unchanged.

# AVENOR — Emote Collection

> 60 individual character emotes, ranked across the same six rarity tiers as weapon skins, kill effects and win effects. Emotes are expressive character animations for the lobby, social spaces and permitted match moments.

## Global presentation rules

- Emotes are cosmetic character animations. They never change movement speed, hitboxes, collision, camera advantage, weapon handling or any gameplay statistic.
- Emotes must be interruptible immediately by movement, aiming, firing, taking damage or other gameplay-critical actions.
- No emote grants invulnerability, hides the character, changes the character's position or blocks other players' view.
- Emotes should read clearly from a normal third-person camera and remain recognizable across different character rigs.
- Each emote has its own name, pose, animation beats and personality. Higher rarity adds more elaborate choreography, props made from cosmetic effects, and bespoke audio, not gameplay power.
- Audio is optional and locally balanced. Emotes must remain understandable with audio disabled.
- Avoid offensive gestures, humiliating portrayals or animations that imitate real-world harm.

## Implementation notes

- Emotes can be equipped individually and placed in a configurable emote wheel.
- Emote wheel selection should be responsive and usable on keyboard, controller and touch.
- Emotes are available in the lobby and social spaces. In active matches, allow only in safe contexts and always cancel immediately when gameplay input or damage occurs.
- Emote animations must not move the character's actual root position or alter collision. Any apparent movement is animation-only.
- Props, holograms and particles are cosmetic attachments with automatic cleanup.
- Team and character variants should preserve the same timing and readability.
- Provide options to mute emote audio and reduce cosmetic visual intensity.
- Higher rarity must never delay respawns, match transitions or access to the next round.
