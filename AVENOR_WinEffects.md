# AVENOR — Win Effect Collection

> 60 individual victory effects, ranked across the same six rarity tiers as weapon skins and kill effects. Win effects celebrate the winning player or team after the match is decided. They are cosmetic presentation only.

## Global presentation rules

- Win effects trigger only after the match result is final. They never activate during live combat.
- The winning player or team remains the visual focus. The result, player names, scores and match information must remain readable.
- Effects should work for solo victories and team victories. Team variants adapt colours and formation without changing the core identity.
- No effect changes rewards, rank points, matchmaking, statistics or gameplay.
- Rarity increases animation complexity, staging, sound design and visual identity. It does not imply a better gameplay result.
- Keep the victory sequence skippable or allow players to reduce its duration. Avoid forced long waits between matches.
- Each effect has its own concept, animation, colour language and sound identity.

## Implementation notes

- Equip one win effect at a time. In team modes, the selected effect can apply to the winning team as a coordinated presentation.
- The effect begins only after the server confirms the final result.
- Keep the final score, player names, team names and progression changes visible throughout.
- Use a shared cleanup and skip system. Higher rarity adds detail, not excessive waiting.
- Provide settings to reduce animation intensity and mute cosmetic audio independently.
- Win effects must never imitate rank rewards, unlock notifications or currency grants in a way that could confuse players.
