# Progression System

FLORA progression separates **collecting plants** from **unlocking the abilities that belong to those plants**.

## Progression layers
- **Plant discovery and ownership:** find a plant in the world and successfully scan it. The first successful scan adds the plant to the permanent collection and grants ownership of its complete ability set.
- **Further research:** investigate an owned plant through meaningful observations, ecological clues and suitable field interactions.
- **Ability unlocks:** research milestones unlock the abilities in that plant's set progressively. The plant page defines the unlock order.
- **Mastery:** continued use and deeper understanding may unlock optional mastery rewards or special interactions.
- **World knowledge:** clues and discoveries can open routes to other regions or worlds.

## Important distinction
- A successful first scan is enough to acquire and own the plant.
- The complete ability set belongs to the player as part of plant ownership.
- Further research unlocks the individual abilities in that set over time.
- A locked ability is still part of the acquired plant's set. It is simply not usable yet.
- Ability description files explain the abilities; they do not define separate ownership or progression.

## Principles
- Progress persists through world rotation.
- Do not make rare plants mandatory for basic progression.
- Reward both exploration and encounter mastery.
- Research tasks should be plant-specific, understandable and varied.
- Avoid repetitive chores, excessive checklists and arbitrary grind.
- Keep exact economy, XP, levels, currencies and reward pacing TBD until the full loop is tested.
