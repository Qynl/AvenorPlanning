# Scanning and Research

Plant acquisition and plant research are connected, but they are not the same thing.

## 1. Find and scan: acquire the plant
1. **Find:** the player discovers a plant growing in the world, in its natural habitat.
2. **Scan:** the player uses the scanner to identify and register the plant.
3. **Acquire:** a successful first scan adds the plant to the player's permanent collection and grants ownership of its complete, predefined ability set.

The first successful scan is enough to acquire the plant. The player does not need to complete a research journey or unlock every ability first.

## 2. Further research: unlock the abilities
After acquiring a plant, the player can continue researching it to unlock its abilities progressively.

1. **Observe:** notice the plant's behaviour, habitat patterns, reactions to weather, nearby organisms or time of day.
2. **Investigate:** follow clues that are specific to that plant.
3. **Experiment:** perform a relevant field interaction or revisit the plant under a meaningful different condition when appropriate.
4. **Research milestones:** completed observations and investigations advance the plant's research and unlock the next ability in the order defined on its canonical plant page.
5. **Master:** deeper research may contribute to optional plant mastery and its rewards.

Research should be specific to the plant. A wind-reactive plant might ask the player to observe it during a gust; a sound-reactive plant might respond to nearby movement or an environmental sound. Do not turn every plant into the same checklist.

## Source of truth
- The plant page defines the research journey and the order in which its abilities unlock.
- Individual ability files describe what each ability does. They are not separate progression systems.
- The Discovery Archive tracks plant ownership, research progress and unlocked abilities as separate pieces of information.

## Rules
- First successful scan grants the plant and its complete ability set.
- Further research unlocks abilities from that set one by one.
- Research never makes the player acquire the same plant again.
- Research progress persists through world rotation.
- Routine scans should be quick. Deeper research should feel like exploration and learning, not repetitive chores.
- Important clues should be readable and logically connected to the plant's ecology.

## Still to design
Exact scanner controls and UI, research milestone counts, task variants, progress thresholds, mastery rewards and numerical pacing remain open for later prototyping. Do not invent a universal research currency or XP grind before the loop is tested.
