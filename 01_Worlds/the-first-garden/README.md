# The First Garden

**World ID:** WORLD_010  
**Status:** PROPOSAL

**Identity:** A mysterious, ancient world that feels like the source of many biological patterns found throughout FLORA.

**Visual palette:** Natural greens and stone tones with restrained luminous accents. It should feel unfamiliar without becoming a generic neon fantasy world.

**Major biomes**
1. **Root of All Paths** — a landscape crossed by enormous branching root structures.
2. **Dawn Chamber** — a sheltered region with unusual light and delicate botanical formations.
3. **The Unwritten Wilds** — an ecosystem that does not match any known world classification.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The First Canopy:** a vast upper ecosystem above the main root network.
- **The Memory Basin:** a quiet region where environmental traces reveal fragments of ancient events.
- **The Seedless Deep:** a concealed region whose organisms do not appear to reproduce in any known way.

**Signature plants**
- Worldroot — Origin.
- Firstlight — Origin.

**Exploration identity:** Long-term research mysteries, multi-world clue chains and discoveries that change how players understand the entire universe.

**Access proposal:** The First Garden should not simply be unlocked by reaching a level. Players should piece together clues from multiple worlds. Exact access conditions and story structure remain TBD.

---

## Rotation and availability proposal

The ten worlds are the initial universe catalogue, not necessarily ten worlds all active at once.

Potential model:
- A smaller set of worlds is active in the main travel network at a time.
- A world rotation introduces a new destination and retires another from the active roster.
- Retired worlds enter an archive or return schedule.
- Important discoveries, permanent unlocks and core progression remain saved.
- Limited-time environmental states can create special discoveries, but the base species catalogue should remain recoverable.
- A world returning can include new sub-biomes, species variants or changed environmental conditions.

**Still undecided:** active world count, rotation duration, whether rotations are global or server-based, and whether archived worlds can be visited on demand.

## Production strategy

Do not build all ten worlds at full size immediately.

Suggested order:
1. Build a polished vertical slice of Auralis.
2. Prove exploration, scanning, plant research, ability unlocks and world streaming.
3. Build one contrasting world such as Umbra Deep or Pelagora to validate the environment pipeline.
4. Establish reusable biome, sub-biome, landmark and discovery systems.
5. Expand the remaining worlds using those proven systems.

## Map production requirements
- Build a readable greybox for each major biome before final art.
- Give each biome a landmark, a main route, an optional route and a hidden discovery route.
- Place the five native species in distinct habitat anchors rather than scattering them randomly.
- Define local streaming regions and test fast traversal across each boundary.
- Keep gameplay-relevant plant clues visible at low graphics quality.
