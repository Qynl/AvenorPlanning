# FLORA: BEYOND THE ORIGINS — Core Gameplay Loop

## Expedition loop
1. Prepare a field loadout from the plants and abilities already unlocked.
2. Enter an active world and choose a destination or lead.
3. Explore landmarks, habitats and hidden routes.
4. Find an unfamiliar plant in its natural environment.
5. Scan the plant. A successful first scan adds the plant to the permanent collection and grants ownership of its complete ability set.
6. Continue exploring and further researching that plant through relevant observations, clues and field interactions.
7. Research milestones progressively unlock individual abilities from that plant's set.
8. Use newly unlocked abilities for traversal, encounters and environmental interactions.
9. Master the plant and follow new clues into deeper regions and other worlds.

## The plant and its ability set
- Every plant has a complete, predefined ability set.
- Scanning acquires the plant and registers the complete set as belonging to the player.
- Further research unlocks the abilities in that set one by one, following the order on the plant's canonical page.
- Ability specification files are descriptions of the individual abilities. They do not define separate progression systems or independently grant ownership.
- The plant page is the source of truth for that plant's research journey and ability unlock order.

## Three connected pillars
- Exploration creates discoveries and opportunities.
- Plant research expands the player's available powers.
- Attacking and encounters give the player reasons to understand, combine and master those powers.

## Design tests
- Does the player have a meaningful reason to leave the main path?
- Does finding and scanning a plant feel rewarding?
- Does further research teach the player something about that plant?
- Does each newly unlocked ability meaningfully expand what the player can do?
- Can a player understand why an ability worked or failed?
- Does the loop create a reason to explore again without relying on repetitive chores?

## Not locked
Exact session length, mission structure, group size, PvE/PvP split, research pacing and loadout switching rules are TBD.
