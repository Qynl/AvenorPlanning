# CRE-001 — Mossback

- **Status:** PROPOSAL
- **Native world:** Auralis
- **Creature type:** Peaceful, large quadruped
- **Signature ability:** Blooming Pollen

## Visual identity

Mossback has a broad, rounded, sturdy body carried by four powerful legs. Its back is a miniature living garden: lush green moss, small plants, delicate flowers and tiny botanical growths. Its head is comparatively small, with gentle expressive eyes. The skin is natural and subtly textured, using earthy colors.

## Distinctive identification cues

- Broad rounded body and four sturdy legs.
- Dense moss and small flowers growing across its back.
- Small head compared with its body.
- Gentle, calm expression.
- Overall impression: a walking moss-covered garden.

Do not confuse it with Rootmaw: Mossback is soft, rounded and covered in living moss, while Rootmaw is heavily bark-armored with root-like limbs.

## Behaviour and ability

Mossback slowly wanders through grasslands and grazes on vegetation. **Blooming Pollen:** glowing golden-green pollen drifts outward from the plants on its back. Grass and nearby vegetation touched by the pollen begin to bloom.

## Habitat cues

Auralis golden grasslands, rolling hills, wildflowers, scattered trees and warm sunlight. Habitat is supporting evidence only.
