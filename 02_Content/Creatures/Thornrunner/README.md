# CRE-002 — Thornrunner

- **Status:** PROPOSAL
- **Native world:** Auralis
- **Creature type:** Fast, agile quadruped
- **Signature ability:** Golden Rush

## Visual identity

Thornrunner has a sleek athletic body, powerful hind legs, narrower front legs and a long flexible balancing tail. Its earthy-golden skin has darker green botanical markings. Several rows of curved, backward-pointing, leaf-shaped thorns run along its spine. It has a streamlined head, expressive eyes and two small branch-like structures above its forehead.

## Distinctive identification cues

- Sleek low-running quadrupedal silhouette.
- Strong hind legs and long balancing tail.
- Rows of backward-curving leaf-like spine thorns.
- Golden and green natural color palette.
- Fast, forward-leaning posture.

Do not confuse it with Mossback: Thornrunner is sleek and thorn-covered, not broad, mossy or flower-covered.

## Behaviour and ability

Thornrunner dashes between grass formations and escapes danger with sudden acceleration. **Golden Rush:** a high-speed burst that leaves a trail of golden leaves and glowing particles. Grass bends outward along its path.

## Habitat cues

Auralis grasslands, rolling hills, wildflowers and warm afternoon light.
