# CRE-007 — Frostmane

- **Status:** PROPOSAL
- **Native world:** Veyr Peaks
- **Creature type:** Large mountain quadruped
- **Signature ability:** Frostwave

## Visual identity

Frostmane is a large quadrupedal creature with thick pale-blue fur, powerful legs and a magnificent flowing mane resembling layered frozen leaves. Crystal-like growths extend from its shoulders and forehead. Its fur transitions from snowy white to icy blue, with subtle glowing patterns. It has expressive eyes and an imposing but appealing silhouette.

## Distinctive identification cues

- Thick pale-blue or snowy-white fur.
- Large flowing mane shaped like frozen leaves.
- Crystal growths on shoulders and forehead.
- Large quadrupedal mountain-animal silhouette.
- Icy blue palette.

## Behaviour and ability

It travels across snowy plateaus and protects smaller creatures. **Frostwave:** a circular wave of sparkling frost energy expands outward, with ice crystals and snow particles briefly frosting nearby surfaces.

## Habitat cues

Veyr Peaks snowy plateaus, icy mountains, frozen trees and drifting snow.
