# Thunderroot

**Plant ID:** PLANT_35  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Relic  
**Home world:** [Veyr Peaks](../../../01_Worlds/veyr-peaks/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Thunder Shelf  
**Ability set:** Stormanchor

## Species identity
Dark roots cling to exposed mountain stone while silver veins pulse before distant thunder.

**Ecological role:** Storm timing and safe electrical paths; it is part of the mountain's weather and traversal ecosystem.

## Discovery and acquisition
**Discovery clue:** Map the timing between its pulses and the storm rolling over the ridge.

Find the plant in the world and successfully scan it. The first successful scan acquires the plant, adds it to the permanent collection and grants ownership of its complete ability set. No further research is required to own the plant.

## Further research
After acquisition, continue investigating this plant through its own ecological clues and field observations. Further research progressively unlocks the abilities in the set, in the order listed below.

Plant-specific research leads:
- Observe the mountain conditions.
- Identify the plant's environmental response.
- Investigate a connected landmark or terrain feature.
- Research the complete ability set.

## Complete ability set
The full set belongs to the player after the first successful scan. Listed order is the ability unlock order; further plant research unlocks each ability progressively. Individual ability files are descriptions only.

| # | Ability | Stable ID | Role | Concept |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Static Sense](../../../03_Content/Abilities/thunderroot/static-sense.md) | PLANT_35_A01 | Control | Reveals nearby charged surfaces and safe grounding points. |
| 2 | [Grounding Root](../../../03_Content/Abilities/thunderroot/grounding-root.md) | PLANT_35_A02 | Control | Creates a temporary safe zone that redirects ambient storm energy into the soil. |
| 3 | [Stormanchor](../../../03_Content/Abilities/thunderroot/stormanchor.md) | PLANT_35_A03 | Control | Links several marked grounding points into a large temporary field that stabilises a storm route. |

| 4 | [Stormheart Convergence](../../../03_Content/Abilities/thunderroot/stormheart-convergence.md) | PLANT_35_A04 | Control | A large temporary storm field links every active grounding point in the region and opens a stable crossing through the ridge. |

## Set presentation
- Primary role: Control
- Theme: Storm timing and safe electrical paths
- Visual language: crystalline needles, sharp mountain silhouettes and clear storm cues.
- Rarity: Relic indicates greater complexity and power.
