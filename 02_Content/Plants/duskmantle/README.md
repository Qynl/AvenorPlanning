# Duskmantle

**Plant ID:** PLANT_37  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Ancient  
**Home world:** [Nacre Hollow](../../../01_Worlds/nacre-hollow/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Drowned Cloister  
**Ability set:** Shadowveil

## Species identity
Broad indigo leaves fold over one another like a cloak, reflecting moonlight at their edges.

**Ecological role:** Soft concealment and reflected light; its presence changes how players read the wetland and its shifting visibility.

## Discovery and acquisition
**Discovery clue:** Find the leaf cluster that remains visible in still water after sunset.

Find the plant in the world and successfully scan it. The first successful scan acquires the plant, adds it to the permanent collection and grants ownership of its complete ability set. No further research is required to own the plant.

## Further research
After acquisition, continue investigating this plant through its own ecological clues and field observations. Further research progressively unlocks the abilities in the set, in the order listed below.

Plant-specific research leads:
- Observe the wetland during different light conditions.
- Identify the species' unique signal.
- Follow a habitat-specific field clue.
- Research the complete ability set.

## Complete ability set
The full set belongs to the player after the first successful scan. Listed order is the ability unlock order; further plant research unlocks each ability progressively. Individual ability files are descriptions only.

| # | Ability | Stable ID | Role | Concept |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Duskveil](../../../03_Content/Abilities/duskmantle/duskveil.md) | PLANT_37_A01 | Stealth and evasion | Briefly softens the player's outline against the environment without granting complete invisibility. |
| 2 | [Mirror Fold](../../../03_Content/Abilities/duskmantle/mirror-fold.md) | PLANT_37_A02 | Stealth and evasion | Creates a stationary leaf reflection that draws attention away from a nearby route. |
| 3 | [Night Mantle](../../../03_Content/Abilities/duskmantle/night-mantle.md) | PLANT_37_A03 | Stealth and evasion | Creates a broad dim-light field that makes movement cues harder for nearby creatures to track. |

## Set presentation
- Primary role: Stealth and evasion
- Theme: Soft concealment and reflected light
- Visual identity: low-contrast indigo, amber lights and reflective water.
- Rarity: Ancient affects the strength and complexity of the set.

## Optional mastery
After the plant's abilities have been unlocked, deeper understanding or continued use may contribute to optional mastery. Exact mastery rewards remain to be designed.
