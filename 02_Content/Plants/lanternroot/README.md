# Lanternroot

**Plant ID:** PLANT_25  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Distinct  
**Home world:** [Myrrwood](../../../01_Worlds/myrrwood/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Lantern Galleries  
**Ability set:** Lumenpath

## Species identity
Pale roots carry warm light through the soil like underground constellations.

**Ecological role:** Light paths and hidden-route discovery; its growth should visibly shape nearby terrain and exploration clues.

## Discovery and acquisition
**Discovery clue:** Trace faint root lights to a hidden junction that only glows when approached quietly.

Find the plant in the world and successfully scan it. The first successful scan acquires the plant, adds it to the permanent collection and grants ownership of its complete ability set. No further research is required to own the plant.

## Further research
After acquisition, continue investigating this plant through its own ecological clues and field observations. Further research progressively unlocks the abilities in the set, in the order listed below.

Plant-specific research leads:
- Notice the species' signature environmental clue.
- Observe its response to player proximity and local conditions.
- Complete a field investigation in its native habitat.
- Record verified findings in the permanent archive.

## Complete ability set
The full set belongs to the player after the first successful scan. Listed order is the ability unlock order; further plant research unlocks each ability progressively. Individual ability files are descriptions only.

| # | Ability | Stable ID | Role | Concept |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Rootglow](../../../03_Content/Abilities/lanternroot/rootglow.md) | PLANT_25_A01 | Exploration | Lights nearby root markings and reveals faint tracks. |
| 2 | [Lantern Line](../../../03_Content/Abilities/lanternroot/lantern-line.md) | PLANT_25_A02 | Exploration | Projects a temporary line of light between two visible root nodes, highlighting a safe route. |
| 3 | [Lumen Gate](../../../03_Content/Abilities/lanternroot/lumen-gate.md) | PLANT_25_A03 | Exploration | Activates a discovered root arch to open a short-lived passage between linked clearings. |

## Set presentation
- Primary role: Exploration
- Theme: Light paths and hidden-route discovery
- Shared visual language: layered bark, warm root-light and natural growth patterns.

## Optional mastery
After the plant's abilities have been unlocked, deeper understanding or continued use may contribute to optional mastery. Exact mastery rewards remain to be designed.
