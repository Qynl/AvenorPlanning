# Pearlcurrent

**Plant ID:** PLANT_32  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Ancient  
**Home world:** [Pelagora](../../../01_Worlds/pelagora/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Pearl Maze  
**Ability set:** Tidal Prism

## Species identity
Pearl-like bulbs connect through translucent ribbons that follow water movement.

**Ecological role:** Water barriers and team movement; the plant signals and subtly alters local weather conditions.

## Discovery and research
Track a pearl's changing position through the maze as the tide turns.
1. Notice the weather clue.
2. Observe the plant during a changing condition.
3. Complete a field investigation on the slope.
4. Record the discovery in the archive.
5. Research the full ability set.
