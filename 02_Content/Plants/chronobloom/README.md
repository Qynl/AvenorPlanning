# Chronobloom

**Plant ID:** PLANT_48  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Origin  
**Home world:** [The First Garden](../../../01_Worlds/the-first-garden/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Stillhour Conservatory  
**Ability set:** Season's Turn

## Species identity
A flower whose petals display different seasonal states at once, rotating without following the sun.

**Ecological role:** Local environmental state and timing; this is an exceptional species connected to the oldest ecological systems in VIREL.

## Discovery and acquisition
**Discovery clue:** Observe its petal cycle and identify the moment when all seasons briefly align.

Find the plant in the world and successfully scan it. The first successful scan acquires the plant, adds it to the permanent collection and grants ownership of its complete ability set. No further research is required to own the plant.

## Further research
After acquisition, continue investigating this plant through its own ecological clues and field observations. Further research progressively unlocks the abilities in the set, in the order listed below.

Plant-specific research leads:
- Identify the unusual environmental signal.
- Observe the specimen across more than one world-state condition.
- Complete a multi-stage field investigation.

## Complete ability set
The full set belongs to the player after the first successful scan. Listed order is the ability unlock order; further plant research unlocks each ability progressively. Individual ability files are descriptions only.

| # | Ability | Stable ID | Role | Concept |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Momentglass](../../../03_Content/Abilities/chronobloom/momentglass.md) | PLANT_48_A01 | Utility | Briefly slows a selected environmental animation or moving natural mechanism. |
| 2 | [Bloom Recall](../../../03_Content/Abilities/chronobloom/bloom-recall.md) | PLANT_48_A02 | Utility | Returns a supported environmental object to its last stable position. |
| 3 | [Season's Turn](../../../03_Content/Abilities/chronobloom/season-s-turn.md) | PLANT_48_A03 | Utility | Temporarily changes a local garden pocket between defined seasonal states, changing its routes and plant responses. |

| 4 | [Era Bloom](../../../03_Content/Abilities/chronobloom/era-bloom.md) | PLANT_48_A04 | Utility | Cycles a marked garden pocket through three defined seasonal states, revealing different routes and plant responses in sequence. |

## Set presentation
- Primary role: Utility
- Theme: Local environmental state and timing
- Visual identity: primordial botanical forms, large readable silhouettes and controlled environmental transformation.
- Rarity: Origin is among the highest rarity tiers; the set should feel exceptional while remaining understandable and counterable.

## Optional mastery
After the plant's abilities have been unlocked, deeper understanding or continued use may contribute to optional mastery. Exact mastery rewards remain to be designed.
