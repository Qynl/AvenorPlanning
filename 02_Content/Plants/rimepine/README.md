# Rimepine

**Plant ID:** PLANT_34  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Ancient  
**Home world:** [Veyr Peaks](../../../01_Worlds/veyr-peaks/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Crowned Glacier  
**Ability set:** Glacier Crown

## Species identity
A compact pine-like plant with crystalline needles that gather frost in geometric fans.

**Ecological role:** Ice shaping and high-altitude routes; it is part of the mountain's weather and traversal ecosystem.

## Discovery and acquisition
**Discovery clue:** Find the only specimen whose needles point toward a hidden warm spring.

Find the plant in the world and successfully scan it. The first successful scan acquires the plant, adds it to the permanent collection and grants ownership of its complete ability set. No further research is required to own the plant.

## Further research
After acquisition, continue investigating this plant through its own ecological clues and field observations. Further research progressively unlocks the abilities in the set, in the order listed below.

Plant-specific research leads:
- Observe the mountain conditions.
- Identify the plant's environmental response.
- Investigate a connected landmark or terrain feature.
- Research the complete ability set.

## Complete ability set
The full set belongs to the player after the first successful scan. Listed order is the ability unlock order; further plant research unlocks each ability progressively. Individual ability files are descriptions only.

| # | Ability | Stable ID | Role | Concept |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Rime Climb](../../../03_Content/Abilities/rimepine/rime-climb.md) | PLANT_34_A01 | Traversal | Creates brief grip points on marked ice surfaces. |
| 2 | [Glacier Step](../../../03_Content/Abilities/rimepine/glacier-step.md) | PLANT_34_A02 | Traversal | Raises a short sequence of sturdy ice platforms along a visible route. |
| 3 | [Crownslide](../../../03_Content/Abilities/rimepine/crownslide.md) | PLANT_34_A03 | Traversal | Creates a controlled ice path down a slope with a clear stopping marker. |

## Set presentation
- Primary role: Traversal
- Theme: Ice shaping and high-altitude routes
- Visual language: crystalline needles, sharp mountain silhouettes and clear storm cues.
- Rarity: Ancient indicates greater complexity and power.

## Optional mastery
After the plant's abilities have been unlocked, deeper understanding or continued use may contribute to optional mastery. Exact mastery rewards remain to be designed.
