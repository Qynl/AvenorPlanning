# Ashwing Fern

**Plant ID:** PLANT_29  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Forgotten  
**Home world:** [Caldera Reach](../../../01_Worlds/caldera-reach/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Sootwind Vents  
**Ability set:** Cinderflight

## Species identity
Dark feathered fronds release glowing ash that spirals upward instead of falling.

**Ecological role:** Thermal lift and aerial control; this plant should visibly interact with the terrain and local climate.

## Discovery and research
Follow an ash plume against the wind to locate its source.
1. Locate the environmental clue.
2. Observe the plant's behaviour.
3. Complete a habitat-specific field investigation.
4. Record the verified discovery.
5. Research its full ability set.
