# Ashwing Fern

**Plant ID:** PLANT_29  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Forgotten  
**Home world:** [Caldera Reach](../../../01_Worlds/caldera-reach/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Sootwind Vents  
**Ability set:** Cinderflight

## Species identity
Dark feathered fronds release glowing ash that spirals upward instead of falling.

**Ecological role:** Thermal lift and aerial control; this plant should visibly interact with the terrain and local climate.

## Discovery and acquisition
**Discovery clue:** Follow an ash plume against the wind to locate its source.

Find the plant in the world and successfully scan it. The first successful scan acquires the plant, adds it to the permanent collection and grants ownership of its complete ability set. No further research is required to own the plant.

## Further research
After acquisition, continue investigating this plant through its own ecological clues and field observations. Further research progressively unlocks the abilities in the set, in the order listed below.

Plant-specific research leads:
- Locate the environmental clue.
- Observe the plant's behaviour.
- Complete a habitat-specific field investigation.
- Research its full ability set.

## Complete ability set
The full set belongs to the player after the first successful scan. Listed order is the ability unlock order; further plant research unlocks each ability progressively. Individual ability files are descriptions only.

| # | Ability | Stable ID | Role | Concept |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Cinder Lift](../../../03_Content/Abilities/ashwing-fern/cinder-lift.md) | PLANT_29_A01 | Movement | Uses a warm updraft for a quick vertical boost. |
| 2 | [Ashtrail](../../../03_Content/Abilities/ashwing-fern/ashtrail.md) | PLANT_29_A02 | Movement | Leaves a brief trail of warm air that can be followed for a second jump. |
| 3 | [Phoenix Drift](../../../03_Content/Abilities/ashwing-fern/phoenix-drift.md) | PLANT_29_A03 | Movement | A long controlled glide powered by a rising thermal column, with a visible landing marker. |

## Set presentation
- Primary role: Movement
- Theme: Thermal lift and aerial control
- Shared visual language: volcanic stone, heat haze and glowing botanical details.
- Rarity: Forgotten signals a more powerful and complex set.
