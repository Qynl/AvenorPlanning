# Sunshard Cactus

**Plant ID:** PLANT_40  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Distinct  
**Home world:** [Sereglass](../../../01_Worlds/sereglass/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Shard Dunes  
**Ability set:** Glassneedle

## Species identity
A tall pale cactus with translucent spines that scatter sunlight into small moving points.

**Ecological role:** Light refraction and route marking; the species changes how players interpret light, distance and terrain.

## Discovery and research
Find the cactus whose shadow points away from the strongest light.
1. Identify the light or shadow clue.
2. Observe the plant from multiple positions.
3. Compare its behaviour with nearby landmarks.
4. Record the confirmed specimen in the archive.
5. Research its full ability set.
