# CRE-009 — Dune Strider

- **Status:** PROPOSAL
- **Native world:** Sereglass
- **Creature type:** Tall desert quadruped
- **Signature ability:** Mirage Spiral

## Visual identity

Dune Strider is an elegant creature with four exceptionally long legs, a narrow body and layered sand-colored armor. Translucent leaf-like structures on its back shimmer in sunlight. Its elongated head has two delicate sensory horns and bright golden eyes. Its leg joints are adapted for walking across loose sand.

## Distinctive identification cues

- Very long, slender legs.
- Narrow body held high above the ground.
- Layered sand-colored armor.
- Translucent leaf-like structures on its back.
- Elongated head, two sensory horns and golden eyes.

## Behaviour and ability

It walks across dunes and uses its height to observe distant surroundings. **Mirage Spiral:** a swirling spiral of sand and golden light forms around its body, with subtle heat-distortion effects and circular glowing particle trails.

## Habitat cues

Sereglass golden dunes, sandstone cliffs, sparse desert vegetation and heat shimmer.
