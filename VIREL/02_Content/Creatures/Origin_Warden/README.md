# CRE-010 — Origin Warden

- **Status:** PROPOSAL
- **Native world:** The First Garden
- **Creature type:** Rare ancient primordial creature
- **Signature ability:** Primordial Bloom

## Visual identity

Origin Warden is an enormous, majestic creature with an elegant but powerful silhouette. Its body combines smooth natural surfaces, ancient golden roots and botanical armor. Large leaf-like structures extend from its back like a crown. Luminous green and golden patterns flow across its body. Its head has branch-like horns and brilliant emerald eyes. It should feel ancient, intelligent and deeply connected to the world.

## Distinctive identification cues

- Majestic, unusually large silhouette.
- Golden roots integrated into its body.
- Crown-like leaf structures rising from its back.
- Flowing green and golden luminous patterns.
- Branch-like horns and emerald eyes.

## Behaviour and ability

It moves slowly through the primordial environment. **Primordial Bloom:** a spectacular wave of botanical energy spreads outward, causing enormous flowers and plants to bloom. Golden-green energy trails, glowing leaves and floating particles surround the creature.

## Habitat cues

The First Garden, enormous ancient trees, extraordinary plants, glowing vegetation and root-formed primordial structures.
