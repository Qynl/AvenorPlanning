# CRE-008 — Mirecrawler

- **Status:** PROPOSAL
- **Native world:** Nacre Hollow
- **Creature type:** Amphibious marsh creature
- **Signature ability:** Luminous Spores

## Visual identity

Mirecrawler has a low body and several flexible limbs. Its exterior is smooth and dark teal, with subtle violet bioluminescent markings. Mushroom-like botanical growths and translucent membranes sit along its back. Its head is wide and distinctive, with luminous eyes and small sensory tendrils. It should look strange and original, but still appealing.

## Distinctive identification cues

- Low, close-to-ground body.
- Several flexible limbs.
- Dark teal surface with violet glowing markings.
- Mushroom-like growths on its back.
- Wide head, luminous eyes and sensory tendrils.

## Behaviour and ability

It moves through shallow water and hides among marsh vegetation. **Luminous Spores:** it releases a cloud of glowing violet and turquoise spores from the growths on its back. The particles drift through mist and reflect in nearby water.

## Habitat cues

Nacre Hollow wetlands, reflective pools, twisted trees, marsh plants and thick atmospheric fog.
