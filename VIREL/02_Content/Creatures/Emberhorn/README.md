# CRE-005 — Emberhorn

- **Status:** PROPOSAL
- **Native world:** Caldera Reach
- **Creature type:** Sturdy volcanic quadruped
- **Signature ability:** Magma Burst

## Visual identity

Emberhorn has a sturdy quadrupedal body protected by dark volcanic stone armor. Glowing orange cracks run across its body. Two large crystal-like horns rise from its head, while jagged obsidian plates cover its back. Small embers glow between armor plates and its eyes are bright amber.

## Distinctive identification cues

- Two prominent crystal-like horns.
- Dark volcanic rock and obsidian armor.
- Bright orange glowing cracks.
- Amber eyes and ember particles.
- Heavy, sturdy quadrupedal silhouette.

## Behaviour and ability

It walks across warm volcanic terrain and gathers near geothermal vents. **Magma Burst:** heated energy erupts from its horns, surrounded by orange sparks and glowing particles. The ground beneath it glows from the heat.

## Habitat cues

Caldera Reach volcanic terrain, obsidian formations, distant lava streams and geothermal vents.
