# Rimepine

**Plant ID:** PLANT_34  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Ancient  
**Home world:** [Veyr Peaks](../../../01_Worlds/veyr-peaks/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Crowned Glacier  
**Ability set:** Glacier Crown

## Species identity
A compact pine-like plant with crystalline needles that gather frost in geometric fans.

**Ecological role:** Ice shaping and high-altitude routes; it is part of the mountain's weather and traversal ecosystem.

## Discovery and research
Find the only specimen whose needles point toward a hidden warm spring.
1. Observe the mountain conditions.
2. Identify the plant's environmental response.
3. Investigate a connected landmark or terrain feature.
4. Record the verified discovery.
5. Research the complete ability set.
