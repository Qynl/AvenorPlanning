# Duskmantle

**Plant ID:** PLANT_37  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Ancient  
**Home world:** [Nacre Hollow](../../../01_Worlds/nacre-hollow/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Drowned Cloister  
**Ability set:** Shadowveil

## Species identity
Broad indigo leaves fold over one another like a cloak, reflecting moonlight at their edges.

**Ecological role:** Soft concealment and reflected light; its presence changes how players read the wetland and its shifting visibility.

## Discovery and research
Find the leaf cluster that remains visible in still water after sunset.
1. Observe the wetland during different light conditions.
2. Identify the species' unique signal.
3. Follow a habitat-specific field clue.
4. Record the verified discovery.
5. Research the complete ability set.
