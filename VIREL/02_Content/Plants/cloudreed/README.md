# Cloudreed

**Plant ID:** PLANT_021  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Unusual  
**Home world:** [Auralis](../../../01_Worlds/auralis/README.md)  
**Habitat:** High wind terraces  
**Ability set:** Skyharp

## Species identity
Tall hollow reeds with pale blue plume tips that sing when air passes through them.

**Ecological role:** Wind currents and sound navigation. This species is part of the local ecosystem, not a decorative collectible only.

## Discovery and field research
Find the reed whose tone changes when the player stands upwind.

1. **Notice:** Identify the environmental clue that distinguishes this species.
2. **Observe:** Scan its appearance and behaviour from more than one useful angle.
3. **Investigate:** Complete a field interaction connected to its habitat.
4. **Record:** Add the verified discovery to the permanent archive.
5. **Research:** Unlock its abilities through the set's milestones.

Exact scan timing, field task and research costs remain TBD.
