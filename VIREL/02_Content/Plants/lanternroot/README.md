# Lanternroot

**Plant ID:** PLANT_25  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Distinct  
**Home world:** [Myrrwood](../../../01_Worlds/myrrwood/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Lantern Galleries  
**Ability set:** Lumenpath

## Species identity
Pale roots carry warm light through the soil like underground constellations.

**Ecological role:** Light paths and hidden-route discovery; its growth should visibly shape nearby terrain and exploration clues.

## Discovery and field research
Trace faint root lights to a hidden junction that only glows when approached quietly.
1. Notice the species' signature environmental clue.
2. Observe its response to player proximity and local conditions.
3. Complete a field investigation in its native habitat.
4. Record verified findings in the permanent archive.
5. Research the full ability set.
