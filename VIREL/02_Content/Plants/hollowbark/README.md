# Hollowbark

**Plant ID:** PLANT_24  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Unusual  
**Home world:** [Myrrwood](../../../01_Worlds/myrrwood/README.md)  
**Habitat:** Rootbound Groves  
**Ability set:** Heartwood

## Species identity
A tree-like plant with a hollow glowing trunk and layered bark plates.

**Ecological role:** Bark structures and protection; its growth should visibly shape nearby terrain and exploration clues.

## Discovery and field research
Listen for the hollow resonance and locate the trunk with a living inner core.
1. Notice the species' signature environmental clue.
2. Observe its response to player proximity and local conditions.
3. Complete a field investigation in its native habitat.
4. Record verified findings in the permanent archive.
5. Research the full ability set.
