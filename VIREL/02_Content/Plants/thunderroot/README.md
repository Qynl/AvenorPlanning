# Thunderroot

**Plant ID:** PLANT_35  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Relic  
**Home world:** [Veyr Peaks](../../../01_Worlds/veyr-peaks/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Thunder Shelf  
**Ability set:** Stormanchor

## Species identity
Dark roots cling to exposed mountain stone while silver veins pulse before distant thunder.

**Ecological role:** Storm timing and safe electrical paths; it is part of the mountain's weather and traversal ecosystem.

## Discovery and research
Map the timing between its pulses and the storm rolling over the ridge.
1. Observe the mountain conditions.
2. Identify the plant's environmental response.
3. Investigate a connected landmark or terrain feature.
4. Record the verified discovery.
5. Research the complete ability set.
