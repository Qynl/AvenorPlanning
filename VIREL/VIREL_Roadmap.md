# VIREL — Production Roadmap

This roadmap is a proposed order of work. Each milestone needs a playable quality gate before the next large content expansion.

## Milestone 0 — Design lock
- Confirm the core fantasy and game pillars.
- Decide camera, movement feel and visual direction.
- Decide whether specimens are physical inventory, archive records or both.
- Define the first-session experience and target session length.
- Set scope boundaries and non-goals.

**Gate:** the game can be explained in one clear paragraph and the core loop is approved.

## Milestone 1 — Core prototype
- Basic player movement and camera.
- Small greybox test area.
- One scanner interaction.
- One complete plant discovery flow.
- One research interaction.
- One simple ability unlock and use.
- Basic save/load proof.

**Gate:** a new player can discover, investigate, research and use one ability without developer help.

## Milestone 2 — Vertical slice
- One polished biome.
- A small but varied species set.
- Multiple discovery conditions.
- Scanner and archive UI.
- Several abilities with different purposes.
- Environmental state prototype.
- Sound, VFX and visual identity pass.
- Mobile and desktop usability tests.

**Gate:** a short expedition is fun, understandable and replayable.

## Milestone 3 — World foundation
- World layout and biome boundaries.
- Terrain and modular environment kit.
- Streaming and memory budgets.
- Landmark and navigation systems.
- Spawn/discovery distribution framework.
- Environmental conditions and local ecology.

**Gate:** the world streams reliably and exploration remains performant.

## Milestone 4 — Content production
- Expand plant catalogue by biome and rarity.
- Add research chains and ability sources.
- Add hidden locations and ecological relationships.
- Add archive completion and mastery rewards.
- Build sound, animation and VFX libraries.

**Gate:** content is varied, discoverable and not dominated by repetitive interactions.

## Milestone 5 — Multiplayer and persistence
- Server-authoritative discovery and rewards.
- Co-op discovery credit rules.
- Save data, reconnect behaviour and data migration.
- Exploit-resistant research and inventory handling.
- Social and party features if approved.

**Gate:** progress is reliable under normal multiplayer conditions.

## Milestone 6 — Alpha
- Full intended launch loop.
- Content breadth across launch biomes.
- Economy only if approved.
- Accessibility, UI and onboarding.
- Performance profiling on low-end mobile devices.
- Bug fixing and exploit testing.

**Gate:** players can understand the game, make progress and return without major blockers.

## Milestone 7 — Beta and launch readiness
- Closed playtests and feedback cycles.
- Difficulty and discovery-rate tuning.
- Server capacity and persistence tests.
- Store page, icons, thumbnails and trailer.
- Moderation and live-operations readiness.

**Gate:** stable launch candidate with a sustainable content plan.

## Milestone 8 — Live development
- New biomes and species.
- Seasonal environmental states.
- Limited-time discoveries with fair return paths.
- New research tools and abilities.
- Community expeditions and quality improvements.

## Production rules
- Prototype systemic risk before mass content production.
- Do not model hundreds of plants before the discovery loop works.
- Every milestone needs explicit acceptance criteria.
- Keep design documents aligned with what is actually implemented.
- Track scope and performance from the first prototype.
