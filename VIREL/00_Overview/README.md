# VIREL — Game Vision

**Name:** VIREL  
**Platform:** Roblox  
**Genre:** Large-scale exploration, botanical discovery, plant-powered abilities and encounters.  
**Camera:** Third-person gameplay with cinematic framing for important discoveries.  
**Visual direction:** Stylized realism.

## High concept
VIREL is a universe of enormous, changing worlds filled with extraordinary plant life. Players explore ecosystems, investigate unknown species, research them to unlock complete ability sets, and use those powers to traverse the world and take part in encounters.

## Three core pillars
1. **Exploration:** enormous worlds, hidden biomes, environmental mysteries and meaningful traversal.
2. **Attacking and encounters:** satisfying, spectacular plant-powered combat. Combat is a core pillar, not an optional side activity.
3. **Plant discovery and powers:** every plant grants a themed multi-ability set, with stronger and more complex sets generally appearing at higher rarities.

## Confirmed direction
- VIREL is separate from AVENOR.
- Stylized realism.
- Multiple enormous worlds with unique ecosystems, biomes and hidden sub-regions.
- Third-person exploration and cinematic discovery presentation.
- Plants are investigated and researched.
- Each plant grants a complete set of multiple abilities.
- Rarity affects gameplay power and typical set scale.
- Exploration, attacking and plant powers are the main pillars.
- The rarity ladder is **Natural → Unusual → Distinct → Ancient → Forgotten → Relic → Origin**.

## Open decisions
PvE enemy roster, PvP, encounter structure, exact ability loadout slots, world rotation schedule, economy, co-op rules, specimen ownership/cultivation and launch scope remain TBD.
