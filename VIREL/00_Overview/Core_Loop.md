# FLORA: BEYOND THE ORIGINS — Core Gameplay Loop

## Expedition loop
1. Prepare a field loadout from researched plant ability sets.
2. Enter an active world and choose a destination or lead.
3. Explore landmarks, habitats and hidden routes.
4. Notice an unfamiliar organism or unusual ecological clue.
5. Scan and investigate it instead of instantly completing a checklist.
6. Perform the relevant observation or field experiment.
7. Record the discovery and earn research progress.
8. Unlock abilities from the plant's set across research milestones.
9. Master the set and use its abilities for traversal, encounters and environmental interactions.
10. Follow new clues into deeper regions and other worlds.

## Three connected pillars
- Exploration creates discoveries and opportunities.
- Plant research expands the player's available powers.
- Attacking and encounters give the player reasons to understand, combine and master those powers.

## Design tests
- Does the player have a meaningful reason to leave the main path?
- Does a discovery teach the player something?
- Does a new plant change how the player can play?
- Can a player understand why an ability worked or failed?
- Does the loop create a reason to explore again without relying on repetitive chores?

## Not locked
Exact session length, mission structure, group size, PvE/PvP split and loadout switching rules are TBD.
