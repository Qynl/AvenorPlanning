# FLORA — Rarity Standard

| Tier | Rarity | Typical set size | Design expectation |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Natural | 2 | Foundational, useful abilities |
| 2 | Unusual | 2–3 | More specialised and expressive |
| 3 | Distinct | 3 | Stronger identity and combinations |
| 4 | Ancient | 3 | Powerful, versatile abilities |
| 5 | Forgotten | 3–4 | High-impact mechanics |
| 6 | Relic | 4 | Very powerful, spectacular sets |
| 7 | Origin | 4+ | Signature mechanics with exceptional identity |

These set sizes are guidelines, not quotas. Plant identity determines the final set. Higher rarity should generally increase gameplay impact, complexity and spectacle. Balance through cooldown, duration, range, conditions, commitment and counterplay. Natural plants must remain useful, and rare plants must not be mandatory for basic progression.
