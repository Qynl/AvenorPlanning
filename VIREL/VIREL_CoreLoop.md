# VIREL — Core Gameplay Loop

## Goal
Make each expedition produce a meaningful discovery, even when the player does not find a top-tier rare plant.

## Primary loop

1. **Prepare:** choose scanner modules, field tools and currently unlocked abilities.
2. **Travel:** explore a large connected world and learn its landmarks, routes and habitats.
3. **Observe:** notice unusual plants, environmental clues, tracks, spores, sounds or growth patterns.
4. **Scan:** identify the organism and reveal available research leads.
5. **Investigate:** use context-appropriate interactions such as observing, sampling, comparing, photographing or testing environmental conditions.
6. **Document:** add the discovery and evidence to the botanical archive.
7. **Research:** complete a research path to reveal properties and possible ability applications.
8. **Apply:** unlock or improve a plant-derived ability, tool function, archive entry or exploration reward.
9. **Return or push onward:** use new knowledge and abilities to reach new habitats and solve new discoveries.

## Make exploration meaningful

- Give players multiple clues rather than a single glowing waypoint.
- Allow some species to be found through observation rather than a map marker.
- Let weather, time, region and local ecology influence discoveries.
- Provide useful discoveries at every rarity tier.
- Reward careful investigation, not only luck.
- Make revisiting a location worthwhile when the player's tools, knowledge or environmental conditions change.

## Expedition rhythm

A good session should contain a mixture of:
- Short discoveries close to a known path.
- Medium investigations requiring a few steps or a return visit.
- Long-form rare discoveries involving several clues or environmental conditions.
- Optional landmarks, hidden areas and scenic points of interest.

## Failure and friction

Research can require thought without becoming tedious. Avoid long unskippable scanning animations, excessive repeated button prompts, arbitrary real-time waits and progress loss from disconnects.
