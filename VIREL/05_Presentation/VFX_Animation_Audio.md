# Animation, VFX and Audio Standard

Every ability should have:
- A recognisable activation animation.
- A distinctive VFX silhouette.
- A satisfying sound identity.
- Clear hit, impact or success feedback.
- Readable area and duration indicators where relevant.
- A quality and performance fallback.

Visual intensity can scale with rarity, but readability is mandatory. Avoid making every ability a generic explosion. Use botanical and environmental visual languages: roots, leaves, spores, wind, water, sound, frost, stone, refraction, light and living energy.
