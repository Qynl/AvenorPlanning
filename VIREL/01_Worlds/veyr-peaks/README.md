# Veyr Peaks

**World ID:** WORLD_005  
**Status:** PROPOSAL

**Identity:** A colossal mountain world with alpine meadows, glaciers, ridges and storm systems.

**Visual palette:** Ice blue, slate grey, pine green, pale lavender and occasional warm gold.

**Major biomes**
1. **Paleglass Shelf** — snowy plateaus and sheltered cold-adapted vegetation.
2. **Thunderline Ridge** — exposed mountain ridges with rapidly changing weather.
3. **Cloudpine Basin** — high-altitude forests and misty valleys.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The Frozen Bellows:** ice caverns with unusual air currents.
- **The Eye of the Storm:** an exposed circular ridge where weather patterns converge.
- **Blue Hour Hollow:** a sheltered valley that remains in cool twilight for much of the day.

**Signature plants**
- Frostwaker — Ancient.
- Stormbell — Forgotten.

**Exploration identity:** Altitude, weather reading, cold conditions, exposed routes and timed environmental clues.
