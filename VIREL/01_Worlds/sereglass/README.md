# Sereglass

**World ID:** WORLD_007  
**Status:** PROPOSAL

**Identity:** A desert world with enormous dunes, weathered stone, dry basins and hidden pockets of water.

**Visual palette:** Sand gold, pale cream, rust red, dusty rose and glassy blue.

**Major biomes**
1. **Drywash Basin** — wide dry riverbeds and scattered stone formations.
2. **Veiled Escarpment** — tall cliffs and heat-shimmering ledges.
3. **Singing Dunes** — rolling dunes with wind-shaped ridges and unusual acoustic patterns.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The Underflow:** a hidden underground watercourse beneath the dry basin.
- **The Veiled Garden:** a concealed habitat behind a heat-distorted rock face.
- **The Glasswind Vault:** a sheltered cavern filled with naturally polished mineral formations.

**Signature plants**
- Pebblecup — Natural.
- Veil Orchid — Ancient.

**Exploration identity:** Reading terrain, locating hidden moisture, navigating heat shimmer and uncovering concealed passages.
