# Myrrwood

**World ID:** WORLD_002  
**Status:** PROPOSAL

**Identity:** An enormous ancient forest with layered canopy, giant trees and interconnected root systems.

**Visual palette:** Deep greens, mossy browns, teal shadows and shafts of warm light.

**Major biomes**
1. **Rootbound Groves** — huge trees and exposed root networks.
2. **Hush Canopy** — elevated branches, hanging gardens and quiet forest clearings.
3. **Fernfall Basin** — dense fern fields around streams and low waterfalls.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The Underbough:** a walkable ecosystem beneath enormous interwoven roots.
- **Hollowheart:** the interior of a colossal fallen tree, containing its own miniature habitat.
- **The Still Canopy:** a secluded upper-forest pocket where the wind barely moves.

**Signature plants**
- Threadroot — Natural.
- Echofern — Distinct.

**Exploration identity:** Vertical routes, subtle environmental clues, interconnected ecosystems and discoveries that reward listening and observation.

## Map production requirements
- Build a readable greybox for each major biome before final art.
- Give each biome a landmark, a main route, an optional route and a hidden discovery route.
- Place the five native species in distinct habitat anchors rather than scattering them randomly.
- Define local streaming regions and test fast traversal across each boundary.
- Keep gameplay-relevant plant clues visible at low graphics quality.
