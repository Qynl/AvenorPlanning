# Pelagora

**World ID:** WORLD_004  
**Status:** PROPOSAL

**Identity:** A huge ocean world with islands, reefs, deep water and submerged ecosystems.

**Visual palette:** Turquoise shallows, rich ocean blue, coral colours and deep indigo.

**Major biomes**
1. **Shallows of Glass** — clear coastal water, tidal flats and small islands.
2. **Bluefall Reef** — layered reefs, arches and bright underwater gardens.
3. **The Open Reach** — broad stretches of ocean connecting distant landforms.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The Sunken Choir:** submerged stone formations that create sounds as currents pass.
- **Kelp Cathedral:** a towering underwater forest with vertical navigation routes.
- **The Still Trench:** a deep, sheltered region where light barely reaches.

**Signature plants**
- Brineglass — Unusual.
- Tideglass — Distinct.

**Exploration identity:** Underwater navigation, depth, currents, visibility and the discovery of habitats hidden below the surface.
