# Quality Gates

## Design gate
- Clear identity and player value.
- Status labels distinguish decisions from proposals.
- Links to related content are valid.

## Gameplay gate
- Mechanic is understandable.
- Ability has a clear purpose and counterplay.
- Discovery teaches something.
- Combat remains readable.

## Presentation gate
- Distinct silhouette, animation, VFX and sound.
- Effects do not obscure critical information.
- Performance fallback exists.

## Technical gate
- Multiplayer state is authoritative where required.
- Data is persistent and recoverable.
- Streaming and memory budgets are respected.
- Mobile and lower-end devices are tested.
