# Content Pipeline

## New world
1. Define world identity, palette and traversal signature.
2. Define major biomes and hidden sub-biomes.
3. Establish environmental variables and landmarks.
4. Assign native plants and discovery clues.
5. Define streaming boundaries and performance budgets.
6. Add links to plant and system documents.

## New plant
1. Give the plant a unique ecological and visual identity.
2. Choose rarity and home habitat.
3. Define how players notice and investigate it.
4. Design a coherent multi-ability set.
5. Create one ability document per ability.
6. Link every ability to the plant and vice versa.
7. Define research milestones and mastery.
8. Test gameplay readability and visual performance.

## New ability
1. Confirm parent plant and set membership.
2. Define gameplay role and pillar.
3. Specify activation, target, effect, limits and counterplay.
4. Specify animation, VFX, sound and feedback.
5. Add research unlock milestone.
6. Test on keyboard/mouse, controller and mobile.
7. Update plant and directory indexes.
