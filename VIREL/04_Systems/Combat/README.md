# Combat and Encounters

**Status:** Attacking is a CONFIRMED core pillar. Exact encounter structure is TBD.

## Combat identity
Combat is built around plant-derived abilities, not a conventional gun arsenal. Each plant set should offer a distinct combat identity while remaining connected to its botanical theme.

## Ability roles
- Focused attacks.
- Area effects and space control.
- Traps, roots and binding.
- Defensive and counter abilities.
- Disruption and utility.
- Movement-based attacks and combinations.

## Encounter design requirements
- Clear telegraphs and readable impact feedback.
- Distinct enemy/target behaviours once the roster is designed.
- Counterplay for powerful abilities.
- Strong spectacle without hiding important information.
- Rarity may increase power and complexity, but must not erase meaningful choices.
- Common abilities remain useful at later stages.
