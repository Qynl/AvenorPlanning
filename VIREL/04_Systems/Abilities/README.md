# Ability System — Shared Rules

## Plant ownership
Every ability belongs to a specific plant and is part of that plant's complete themed set. The parent plant is the source of truth for set identity and unlock progression.

## Ability pillars
- **Exploration:** traversal, sensing, navigation and access.
- **Attacking:** direct attacks, area control, traps and disruption.
- **Botanical powers:** plant growth, environmental shaping and interactions.

## Rarity scaling
The seven tiers are Natural, Unusual, Distinct, Ancient, Forgotten, Relic and Origin. Higher rarity should generally increase the strength, versatility, complexity and spectacle of a plant's complete set. Do not make rarity a cosmetic-only label. Balance powerful sets through cooldown, duration, commitment, range, counterplay and situational limits. Lower-rarity plants must remain useful.

The current content catalogue proposes three abilities per plant as a consistent starting structure. This is a production baseline, not a permanent quota; future plants may justify a different set size when their identity needs it.

## Set ownership and unlocks
- A plant owns its full set and its research order.
- Players research a plant before unlocking its abilities.
- Ability pages define mechanics; plant pages define set identity and progression.
- Avoid designing three unrelated abilities just to fill a count. Every set needs a coherent theme.

## Required ability specification
Every ability must define:
- Stable ID and display name.
- Parent plant and ability set.
- Primary pillar and gameplay role.
- Activation and target rules.
- Effect and duration.
- Range/area and cooldown placeholders.
- Counterplay and limitations.
- Animation, VFX, sound and feedback.
- Accessibility/readability considerations.
- Research unlock milestone.

Exact numerical values are tuning data and remain TBD until playtesting.
