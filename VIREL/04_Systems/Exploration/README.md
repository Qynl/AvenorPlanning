# Exploration System

**Purpose:** Make exploration the main attraction and reward curiosity, observation and mastery.

## Design requirements
- Large readable spaces with strong landmarks and multiple route choices.
- Vertical, underground, underwater, canopy and surface traversal where appropriate.
- Hidden areas communicate clues rather than relying on random searching.
- Plant abilities can unlock routes, reveal traces or change traversal options.
- Returning with new knowledge or abilities can reveal new paths.
- Environmental conditions should be legible and learnable.

## Open decisions
Map UI, navigation assists, fast travel, expedition structure, group scaling and exact world dimensions remain TBD.
