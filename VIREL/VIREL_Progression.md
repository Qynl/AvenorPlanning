# VIREL — Progression and Collection

## Progression philosophy
Progress should represent curiosity, research knowledge and exploration. Avoid turning the game into a repetitive currency treadmill.

## Parallel progression tracks

### 1. Botanical Archive
- Species discovered.
- Scan and research completion.
- Variants and unusual states recorded.
- Habitat knowledge and ecological links.
- Completion milestones and archive presentation.

### 2. Research Rank
A broad measure of documented scientific progress. Rank rewards can include scanner functions, research access, titles and cosmetic identity. Rank should not be a pay-to-win power score.

### 3. Ability Knowledge
Tracks plant-derived abilities discovered and mastered. Players should understand the source and requirements for each ability.

### 4. Exploration Record
Tracks regions visited, landmarks found, secrets solved and expedition milestones.

## Rewards

Potential rewards:
- New abilities or ability variants.
- Scanner modules.
- Archive pages and scientific notes.
- Titles, badges and profile decorations.
- Specimen display options.
- Character and equipment cosmetics.
- Access clues to optional discoveries.

## Collection quality

The encyclopedia should distinguish:
- Seen.
- Detected.
- Documented.
- Studied.
- Mastered.
- Variant discovered.

Give players useful completion goals without requiring every species to be mastered before they can enjoy the world.

## Persistence

Discovery records, research progress and permanent unlocks should persist across sessions. Exact data schema, trading, specimen loss and event rewards are TBD.

## Economy

No currency, shop, trading or monetisation system is confirmed. If introduced, paid products must not sell exclusive gameplay power or remove the value of exploration.
