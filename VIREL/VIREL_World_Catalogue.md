# VIREL — World Catalogue

## Purpose and status
This document proposes the first **10 enormous worlds** for VIREL. Each world has a strong visual identity, several major biomes and hidden sub-biomes that reward exploration.

The ten world concepts and their names are proposals. The seven rarity names are confirmed. The initial 20 signature plants are mapped to these worlds in [VIREL_Plants.md](VIREL_Plants.md).

## Shared world design rules

- Each world must feel distinct from the others in silhouette, palette, atmosphere, traversal and ecology.
- Each world has at least three major biomes and multiple sub-biomes.
- A sub-biome should not just be a recoloured room. It needs a distinct ecosystem, environmental condition, landmark or discovery pattern.
- Every world should contain discoveries across several rarity levels over time. Do not reserve all interesting content for endgame worlds.
- Hidden regions should be discoverable through clues, observation, environmental interaction or abilities.
- World rotation must not permanently erase core progression. Returning worlds, archives or other fair recovery systems are required.
- Build each world from streaming-friendly regions rather than one unbounded map.

## 01. Auralis
**Identity:** A vast, welcoming world of open grasslands, warm light and enormous distant horizons.

**Visual palette:** Fresh greens, warm gold, pale blue skies and soft white stone.

**Major biomes**
1. **Windstep Prairie** — rolling grasslands, long ridges and wind-shaped vegetation.
2. **Sunthread Fields** — bright meadows with spiral flower beds and scattered groves.
3. **Glasswater Basin** — shallow lakes, reed beds and reflective streams.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The Whispering Hollows:** shallow valleys where wind creates unusual tones.
- **Rainmirror Pools:** small pools that appear after heavy rain and reflect distant landmarks.
- **The Buried Meadow:** a hidden underground pocket beneath the prairie, reached through a collapsed root tunnel.

**Signature plants**
- Driftleaf — Natural.
- Sunspool — Natural.

**Exploration identity:** The first large-scale expedition world. Teaches reading wind, light, terrain and plant behaviour without feeling like a tiny tutorial map.

## 02. Myrrwood
**Identity:** An enormous ancient forest with layered canopy, giant trees and interconnected root systems.

**Visual palette:** Deep greens, mossy browns, teal shadows and shafts of warm light.

**Major biomes**
1. **Rootbound Groves** — huge trees and exposed root networks.
2. **Hush Canopy** — elevated branches, hanging gardens and quiet forest clearings.
3. **Fernfall Basin** — dense fern fields around streams and low waterfalls.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The Underbough:** a walkable ecosystem beneath enormous interwoven roots.
- **Hollowheart:** the interior of a colossal fallen tree, containing its own miniature habitat.
- **The Still Canopy:** a secluded upper-forest pocket where the wind barely moves.

**Signature plants**
- Threadroot — Natural.
- Echofern — Distinct.

**Exploration identity:** Vertical routes, subtle environmental clues, interconnected ecosystems and discoveries that reward listening and observation.

## 03. Caldera Reach
**Identity:** A volcanic world where life thrives around cooled lava, mineral channels and geothermal activity.

**Visual palette:** Basalt black, copper, ember orange, muted red and occasional turquoise mineral deposits.

**Major biomes**
1. **Cinder Veins** — cooled lava channels and warm mineral ground.
2. **Ashen Terraces** — layered volcanic slopes with sparse resilient vegetation.
3. **Obsidian Fields** — dark glass-like rock formations and narrow ravines.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The Buried Furnace:** a deep geothermal chamber with old mineral structures.
- **Steamglass Tunnels:** narrow passages shaped by ancient steam vents.
- **The Quiet Caldera:** a sheltered crater where unusual life grows around cooled rock.

**Signature plants**
- Emberlace — Distinct.
- Ashen Crown — Relic.

**Exploration identity:** Environmental hazards, heat patterns, geological clues and careful route selection. The world should feel dangerous-looking without relying on combat.

## 04. Pelagora
**Identity:** A huge ocean world with islands, reefs, deep water and submerged ecosystems.

**Visual palette:** Turquoise shallows, rich ocean blue, coral colours and deep indigo.

**Major biomes**
1. **Shallows of Glass** — clear coastal water, tidal flats and small islands.
2. **Bluefall Reef** — layered reefs, arches and bright underwater gardens.
3. **The Open Reach** — broad stretches of ocean connecting distant landforms.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The Sunken Choir:** submerged stone formations that create sounds as currents pass.
- **Kelp Cathedral:** a towering underwater forest with vertical navigation routes.
- **The Still Trench:** a deep, sheltered region where light barely reaches.

**Signature plants**
- Brineglass — Unusual.
- Tideglass — Distinct.

**Exploration identity:** Underwater navigation, depth, currents, visibility and the discovery of habitats hidden below the surface.

## 05. Veyr Peaks
**Identity:** A colossal mountain world with alpine meadows, glaciers, ridges and storm systems.

**Visual palette:** Ice blue, slate grey, pine green, pale lavender and occasional warm gold.

**Major biomes**
1. **Paleglass Shelf** — snowy plateaus and sheltered cold-adapted vegetation.
2. **Thunderline Ridge** — exposed mountain ridges with rapidly changing weather.
3. **Cloudpine Basin** — high-altitude forests and misty valleys.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The Frozen Bellows:** ice caverns with unusual air currents.
- **The Eye of the Storm:** an exposed circular ridge where weather patterns converge.
- **Blue Hour Hollow:** a sheltered valley that remains in cool twilight for much of the day.

**Signature plants**
- Frostwaker — Ancient.
- Stormbell — Forgotten.

**Exploration identity:** Altitude, weather reading, cold conditions, exposed routes and timed environmental clues.

## 06. Nacre Hollow
**Identity:** A mysterious wetland world of shallow water, drifting fog, reflective pools and dense marsh vegetation.

**Visual palette:** Muted jade, pearl white, deep teal, soft violet and silver mist.

**Major biomes**
1. **Drifting Marsh** — broad wetlands with islands of vegetation.
2. **Nightglass Fen** — dark pools, reflective water and nocturnal life.
3. **Pearlreed Flats** — open reed beds and shallow winding channels.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The Fog Maze:** a shifting network of narrow channels whose landmarks are easier to read through plant clues.
- **Drowned Orchard:** the remains of an ancient grove beneath shallow water.
- **The Quiet Mirror:** a hidden pool where the water surface remains unusually still.

**Signature plants**
- Mistbell — Unusual.
- Moonlace — Forgotten.

**Exploration identity:** Visibility, night exploration, moisture trails, subtle sound cues and returning at different times.

## 07. Sereglass
**Identity:** A desert world with enormous dunes, weathered stone, dry basins and hidden pockets of water.

**Visual palette:** Sand gold, pale cream, rust red, dusty rose and glassy blue.

**Major biomes**
1. **Drywash Basin** — wide dry riverbeds and scattered stone formations.
2. **Veiled Escarpment** — tall cliffs and heat-shimmering ledges.
3. **Singing Dunes** — rolling dunes with wind-shaped ridges and unusual acoustic patterns.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The Underflow:** a hidden underground watercourse beneath the dry basin.
- **The Veiled Garden:** a concealed habitat behind a heat-distorted rock face.
- **The Glasswind Vault:** a sheltered cavern filled with naturally polished mineral formations.

**Signature plants**
- Pebblecup — Natural.
- Veil Orchid — Ancient.

**Exploration identity:** Reading terrain, locating hidden moisture, navigating heat shimmer and uncovering concealed passages.

## 08. Umbra Deep
**Identity:** A vast subterranean world with enormous caverns, vertical rock formations and independent underground ecosystems.

**Visual palette:** Deep navy, charcoal, muted teal, mineral violet and carefully placed bioluminescent light.

**Major biomes**
1. **Lantern Hollows** — caverns with naturally glowing organisms.
2. **The Vertical Archive** — enormous shafts, ledges and layered stone walls.
3. **Blackwater Galleries** — underground lakes and submerged passages.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The Root Cathedral:** a cavern crossed by giant roots descending from the surface.
- **The Crystal Lung:** a chamber where mineral formations create slow, visible air currents.
- **The Blind Garden:** a hidden ecosystem with almost no natural light.

**Signature plants**
- Glowcap — Unusual.
- Stoneheart Moss — Ancient.

**Exploration identity:** Darkness, vertical navigation, cave mapping, sound, biological light and hidden underground ecosystems.

## 09. Zephyr Crown
**Identity:** A high-altitude world of immense plateaus, wind-carved cliffs, suspended-looking rock formations and rare aerial routes.

**Visual palette:** Sky blue, pale stone, cloud white, soft green and occasional violet.

**Major biomes**
1. **Windwoven Cliffs** — exposed cliffs and narrow natural anchor points.
2. **The High Aeries** — broad elevated plateaus and unusual air currents.
3. **Cloudgarden Fields** — open highland meadows above the cloud line.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The Updraft Wells:** deep vertical openings where air rises in powerful columns.
- **The Hanging Grove:** a sheltered forest growing across connected cliff ledges.
- **The Farwind Shelf:** a remote plateau reachable through a chain of environmental traversal challenges.

**Signature plants**
- Springvine — Unusual.
- Skydrift — Relic.

**Exploration identity:** Precision movement, wind reading, vertical traversal and routes that become accessible through learned abilities.

## 10. The First Garden
**Identity:** A mysterious, ancient world that feels like the source of many biological patterns found throughout VIREL.

**Visual palette:** Natural greens and stone tones with restrained luminous accents. It should feel unfamiliar without becoming a generic neon fantasy world.

**Major biomes**
1. **Root of All Paths** — a landscape crossed by enormous branching root structures.
2. **Dawn Chamber** — a sheltered region with unusual light and delicate botanical formations.
3. **The Unwritten Wilds** — an ecosystem that does not match any known world classification.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The First Canopy:** a vast upper ecosystem above the main root network.
- **The Memory Basin:** a quiet region where environmental traces reveal fragments of ancient events.
- **The Seedless Deep:** a concealed region whose organisms do not appear to reproduce in any known way.

**Signature plants**
- Worldroot — Origin.
- Firstlight — Origin.

**Exploration identity:** Long-term research mysteries, multi-world clue chains and discoveries that change how players understand the entire universe.

**Access proposal:** The First Garden should not simply be unlocked by reaching a level. Players should piece together clues from multiple worlds. Exact access conditions and story structure remain TBD.

## Rotation and availability proposal

The ten worlds are the initial universe catalogue, not necessarily ten worlds all active at once.

Potential model:
- A smaller set of worlds is active in the main travel network at a time.
- A world rotation introduces a new destination and retires another from the active roster.
- Retired worlds enter an archive or return schedule.
- Important discoveries, permanent unlocks and core progression remain saved.
- Limited-time environmental states can create special discoveries, but the base species catalogue should remain recoverable.
- A world returning can include new sub-biomes, species variants or changed environmental conditions.

**Still undecided:** active world count, rotation duration, whether rotations are global or server-based, and whether archived worlds can be visited on demand.

## Production strategy

Do not build all ten worlds at full size immediately.

Suggested order:
1. Build a polished vertical slice of Auralis.
2. Prove exploration, scanning, plant research, ability unlocks and world streaming.
3. Build one contrasting world such as Umbra Deep or Pelagora to validate the environment pipeline.
4. Establish reusable biome, sub-biome, landmark and discovery systems.
5. Expand the remaining worlds using those proven systems.
