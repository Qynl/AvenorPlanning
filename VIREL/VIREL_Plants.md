# VIREL — Plant Catalogue and Rarity

## Status
**The seven rarity names are confirmed by the user.** The 20 species below and their abilities are the first full catalogue proposal. Names, habitats and mechanics can still be revised.

## Rarity ladder

| Order | Rarity | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **Natural** | Common, foundational species that make each ecosystem feel alive. |
| 2 | **Unusual** | Less common species with distinctive adaptations or interactions. |
| 3 | **Distinct** | Rare species with especially recognisable biological traits. |
| 4 | **Ancient** | Species connected to old ecosystems, deep time or unusual geological conditions. |
| 5 | **Forgotten** | Extremely uncommon species whose existence is poorly documented. |
| 6 | **Relic** | Exceptional organisms that appear to preserve traits from another age. |
| 7 | **Origin** | Singular discoveries tied to the deepest mysteries of VIREL's worlds. |

Rarity communicates discovery difficulty, uniqueness and research significance. It does **not** directly determine ability strength. A Natural plant can unlock a frequently useful traversal tool; an Origin discovery can reveal knowledge or a world-scale secret rather than grant an overpowered movement ability.

## Species dossiers

### 01. Driftleaf
- **Rarity:** Natural
- **Habitat:** Auralis, Windstep Prairie.
- **Appearance:** Broad, thin leaves with pale green surfaces and silver undersides. The leaves tilt together when a strong gust approaches.
- **Discovery clue:** Look for patches that all lean in the same direction before the wind changes.
- **Research:** Observe its leaf angle during several wind strengths.
- **Ability — Softfall:** A controlled descent that slows falling and allows gentle steering. It does not provide unlimited flight.
- **Role:** An early, reliable traversal ability.

### 02. Sunspool
- **Rarity:** Natural
- **Habitat:** Auralis, Sunthread Fields.
- **Appearance:** Small golden flowers arranged in spiral clusters that slowly turn toward the brightest light.
- **Discovery clue:** Follow the direction of several flower heads to find the densest patch.
- **Research:** Compare its orientation at different times of day.
- **Research outcome — Solar Reading:** Adds sun exposure and warmth information to the field scanner. It is a useful research tool, not a movement power.
- **Role:** Teaches players to read environmental conditions.

### 03. Threadroot
- **Rarity:** Natural
- **Habitat:** Myrrwood, Rootbound Groves.
- **Appearance:** Fine copper-green roots that travel over the soil and connect nearby trees.
- **Discovery clue:** Find clusters of trees whose roots form repeating braided patterns.
- **Research:** Trace a connected root network without damaging it.
- **Ability — Rootsense:** Briefly highlights nearby connected root networks and certain plant signals.
- **Role:** Helps players understand ecosystem connections.

### 04. Echofern
- **Rarity:** Distinct
- **Habitat:** Myrrwood, Hush Canopy.
- **Appearance:** Layered blue-green fronds with tiny reflective spots that shimmer after movement nearby.
- **Discovery clue:** Look for fronds that react a moment after an animal or environmental movement.
- **Research:** Compare its response to wind, footsteps and nearby wildlife.
- **Ability — Echo Trace:** Briefly visualises recent environmental traces in the nearby area. It shows clues, not exact player locations.
- **Role:** Investigation and discovery support.

### 05. Emberlace
- **Rarity:** Distinct
- **Habitat:** Caldera Reach, Cinder Veins.
- **Appearance:** Dark red filaments surrounding a warm amber centre. It opens when geothermal heat rises.
- **Discovery clue:** Search the edges of warm mineral channels rather than the hottest ground.
- **Research:** Record its opening pattern as nearby ground temperature changes.
- **Ability — Heatstep:** Temporarily protects the player while crossing designated hot surfaces. It does not make every volcanic hazard harmless.
- **Role:** A situational environmental adaptation.

### 06. Ashen Crown
- **Rarity:** Relic
- **Habitat:** Caldera Reach, The Buried Furnace.
- **Appearance:** A low, crown-shaped organism with charcoal petals and faint glowing seams. Its oldest specimens grow around mineral formations.
- **Discovery clue:** Follow cooled lava channels to unusual mineral rings.
- **Research:** Map the mineral pattern around the plant and compare it with nearby rock layers.
- **Research outcome — Deep-Time Survey:** Reveals old geothermal routes and geological archive entries. It does not grant a direct power.
- **Role:** A major lore and world-understanding discovery.

### 07. Brineglass
- **Rarity:** Unusual
- **Habitat:** Pelagora, Shallows of Glass.
- **Appearance:** Translucent ribbon-like leaves with a blue-green edge. Their direction shifts with underwater currents.
- **Discovery clue:** Watch for ribbons pointing against the visible surface current.
- **Research:** Track its orientation at different depths.
- **Research outcome — Current Reading:** Adds local current direction and strength to the underwater scanner.
- **Role:** Navigation utility for underwater expeditions.

### 08. Tideglass
- **Rarity:** Distinct
- **Habitat:** Pelagora, Bluefall Reef.
- **Appearance:** Clear, fan-shaped leaves with refractive edges that scatter sunlight into narrow bands.
- **Discovery clue:** Search reef ledges where sunlight reaches through moving water.
- **Research:** Observe how the plant flexes during changing currents.
- **Ability — Deep Current:** Improves underwater movement and control for a limited period. It does not remove the need to navigate underwater routes.
- **Role:** Opens up deeper and more complex aquatic exploration.

### 09. Frostwaker
- **Rarity:** Ancient
- **Habitat:** Veyr Peaks, Paleglass Shelf.
- **Appearance:** Dense, pale leaves folded around a dark central bud. It releases a faint mist when exposed to cold air.
- **Discovery clue:** Find sheltered ledges where snow gathers in unusual circular patterns.
- **Research:** Observe how its leaves respond to temperature shifts.
- **Ability — Coldbound:** Provides temporary protection from designated cold conditions and reveals nearby warm refuges.
- **Role:** Makes high-altitude routes more manageable.

### 10. Stormbell
- **Rarity:** Forgotten
- **Habitat:** Veyr Peaks, Thunderline Ridge.
- **Appearance:** Hanging bell-shaped blooms with thin metallic-looking stems. They vibrate before distant storms.
- **Discovery clue:** Listen for a low ringing when the sky still appears clear.
- **Research:** Record its vibration pattern before and during changing weather.
- **Research outcome — Storm Forecast:** Adds local storm warnings and weather-sensitive discovery clues to the scanner.
- **Role:** A high-value information discovery.

### 11. Mistbell
- **Rarity:** Unusual
- **Habitat:** Nacre Hollow, Drifting Marsh.
- **Appearance:** Soft ivory bells surrounded by droplets that remain suspended close to the petals.
- **Discovery clue:** Search where fog gathers above shallow water and low vegetation.
- **Research:** Follow the droplets as humidity changes.
- **Ability — Moisture Sense:** Briefly highlights moisture trails, hidden pools and damp passages nearby.
- **Role:** Helps locate hidden wetland routes.

### 12. Moonlace
- **Rarity:** Forgotten
- **Habitat:** Nacre Hollow, Nightglass Fen.
- **Appearance:** Fine dark leaves with pale threadlike veins. Small flowers open only under low natural light.
- **Discovery clue:** Visit the fen after dusk and follow the faint patterns between flowers.
- **Research:** Compare its response to moonlight, cloud cover and artificial light.
- **Ability — Nocturne Sight:** Makes nocturnal organisms and their recent traces easier to notice for a short time.
- **Role:** Rewards returning to familiar places under different conditions.

### 13. Pebblecup
- **Rarity:** Natural
- **Habitat:** Sereglass, Drywash Basin.
- **Appearance:** Small stone-coloured cups that collect condensation inside their hollow centres.
- **Discovery clue:** Inspect the shaded side of rock clusters at dawn.
- **Research:** Measure how much condensation different cup shapes retain.
- **Research outcome — Moisture Mapping:** Adds nearby hidden moisture pockets to the scanner.
- **Role:** A simple species with practical exploration value.

### 14. Veil Orchid
- **Rarity:** Ancient
- **Habitat:** Sereglass, The Veiled Escarpment.
- **Appearance:** Long translucent petals that blend into heat shimmer when viewed from a distance.
- **Discovery clue:** Search for a distortion in the air that does not match the surrounding heat.
- **Research:** Observe the flower from several angles and record how the background appears through its petals.
- **Ability — Veil Sight:** Briefly reveals concealed natural passages and hidden markings. It does not reveal every secret automatically.
- **Role:** Unlocks optional secrets and alternate routes.

### 15. Glowcap
- **Rarity:** Unusual
- **Habitat:** Umbra Deep, Lantern Hollows.
- **Appearance:** Rounded caps with soft internal light and tiny drifting spores.
- **Discovery clue:** Follow faint light pulses deeper into the cave system.
- **Research:** Observe how the glow changes near other organisms.
- **Ability — Living Light:** Creates a small, controllable biological light source. Its brightness and duration are limited.
- **Role:** A practical early cave-exploration tool.

### 16. Stoneheart Moss
- **Rarity:** Ancient
- **Habitat:** Umbra Deep, The Vertical Archive.
- **Appearance:** Thick dark moss with pale crystalline flecks, growing in narrow bands across steep stone.
- **Discovery clue:** Look for patches growing in places where ordinary moss would slide away.
- **Research:** Study the attachment pattern on different rock types.
- **Ability — Stonegrip:** Allows the player to cling briefly to designated natural climbing surfaces.
- **Role:** Adds vertical routes and secret climbing paths.

### 17. Springvine
- **Rarity:** Unusual
- **Habitat:** Zephyr Crown, Windwoven Cliffs.
- **Appearance:** Long flexible vines with spring-like coils and bright leaf tips.
- **Discovery clue:** Look for vines that recoil after strong gusts.
- **Research:** Observe how the coils respond to tension and wind direction.
- **Ability — Vinepull:** Pulls the player toward suitable natural anchor points. It cannot attach to arbitrary surfaces.
- **Role:** A skill-based traversal tool.

### 18. Skydrift
- **Rarity:** Relic
- **Habitat:** Zephyr Crown, The High Aeries.
- **Appearance:** A floating cluster of fine leaf structures that slowly changes shape as air currents pass through it.
- **Discovery clue:** Search high-altitude pockets where loose leaves circle without falling.
- **Research:** Track its shape in several wind conditions.
- **Ability — Airstep:** Creates one brief midair foothold, allowing a carefully timed extra jump. It has a clear cooldown and cannot chain indefinitely.
- **Role:** A precision traversal ability.

### 19. Worldroot
- **Rarity:** Origin
- **Habitat:** The First Garden, Root of All Paths.
- **Appearance:** An immense living root structure with branching patterns that do not match any known species.
- **Discovery clue:** Its location is not shown directly. Players must connect clues from several worlds.
- **Research:** Document its branching pattern and compare it with root structures recorded elsewhere.
- **Ability — Rootway:** Activates specific ancient root gates that connect established hidden locations. It does not allow unrestricted teleportation.
- **Role:** A world-scale discovery and late-game navigation system.

### 20. Firstlight
- **Rarity:** Origin
- **Habitat:** The First Garden, Dawn Chamber.
- **Appearance:** A delicate flower with layered translucent petals. Its colour shifts subtly as the player approaches.
- **Discovery clue:** Requires multiple research records from other worlds; the exact puzzle is TBD.
- **Research:** Combine biological traces from several Origin-era records to understand its response.
- **Research outcome — Genesis Record:** Reveals deep biological memories and major pieces of VIREL's underlying history. It is a knowledge milestone, not a raw power upgrade.
- **Role:** One of the most important discoveries in the game.

## Ability design rules

- Not every plant grants an ability. Research tools, scanner upgrades, archive entries and world clues are equally valid rewards.
- Abilities should have a clear use, readable feedback and a counterbalance such as duration, cooldown, range or eligible surfaces.
- Avoid making a rare ability mandatory for basic progression.
- Give players alternate routes where an ability reveals a shortcut.
- Abilities should be tested on keyboard/mouse, controller and mobile.
- Rarity does not equal raw power.

## Collection and research assumptions

**Proposal:** Players document species in a permanent field archive. Some interactions may produce research samples, but the player should not need to destroy or permanently remove every plant they discover. Physical specimen collection, trading, cultivation and display systems remain undecided.

## Future expansion

The 20 plants are a launch-foundation catalogue, not the total planned number of species. Future additions can include:
- Variants that respond to weather, time or world state.
- Symbiotic pairs that reveal a third discovery when studied together.
- Rare growth stages.
- Mobile or migrating botanical organisms.
- Species that can only be identified through sound, tracks or environmental changes.
- Multi-world research chains.
