# VIREL — Plant Catalogue and Rarity

## Status
The seven rarity names are confirmed. The 20 plants below are the first catalogue proposal. Their ability sets establish the new direction: **each discovered plant unlocks a set of multiple abilities**, not one isolated perk.

## Rarity ladder and ability-set scale

| Order | Rarity | Identity | Typical set size | Power expectation |
|---:|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | **Natural** | Foundational species of each ecosystem | 2 | Useful, accessible abilities |
| 2 | **Unusual** | Noticeably unusual adaptations | 2–3 | More specialised and expressive |
| 3 | **Distinct** | Rare, recognisable organisms | 3 | Stronger effects and combinations |
| 4 | **Ancient** | Species tied to old ecosystems | 3 | Powerful, versatile abilities |
| 5 | **Forgotten** | Extremely uncommon, poorly documented species | 3–4 | High-impact abilities with unusual mechanics |
| 6 | **Relic** | Exceptional organisms preserving traits from another age | 4 | Very powerful, visually spectacular sets |
| 7 | **Origin** | Singular discoveries tied to VIREL's deepest mysteries | 4+ | Signature, potentially world-changing abilities |

Rarity now **does affect ability strength and set scale**. Higher-rarity plants generally provide stronger, more complex and more visually spectacular sets. The exact number depends on the plant's identity, not a rigid quota. Balance should come from cooldowns, duration, range, conditions, commitment and counterplay, not by making rare plants feel weak.

## The three ability pillars

Every ability set should contribute to one or more of these pillars:

1. **Exploration:** movement, traversal, reaching hidden places, sensing clues and navigating environments.
2. **Attacking:** spectacular plant-powered attacks for encounters. Exact enemy types, PvP and combat rules remain open.
3. **Botanical powers:** creating, controlling, shaping or interacting with plant life and the environment.

Not every plant must cover all three pillars. Each should have a coherent theme and a set that feels like it belongs to that species.

Ability names and mechanics below are proposals. Each set is designed around a distinct visual language. The full animation, VFX, sound and balance specifications will be developed separately.

## Ability presentation standard

Every ability must feel special when used, not only when unlocked.

Each ability needs:
- A recognisable activation animation.
- Distinctive VFX that communicate its shape, range and impact.
- A satisfying sound identity.
- Clear feedback for hit, miss, activation, cooldown and environmental interaction.
- A strong visual silhouette that remains readable in busy multiplayer scenes.
- A memorable signature moment for each plant set.

Rare sets should have increasingly elaborate effects, but visual clarity takes priority over screen-filling clutter. Do not make every ability a giant explosion; use different visual languages such as roots, petals, spores, light, water, wind, sound, frost and stone.

## Ability-set design rules

- A plant grants a set, not a single ability.
- Set size and power generally scale with rarity, while plant identity determines the exact abilities.
- Sets can mix exploration, attacking and botanical/environmental utility.
- A plant does not need to cover every pillar.
- Rare abilities should feel genuinely stronger and more spectacular, with balance through cooldown, duration, range, conditions and commitment.
- Keep core exploration possible without owning a particular rare plant.
- Abilities must be readable and responsive on keyboard/mouse, controller and mobile.

## Collection and research assumptions

Players document discoveries in a permanent field archive. Research unlocks the complete ability set over meaningful milestones rather than handing every ability over instantly. Physical specimen collection, trading, cultivation and display systems remain undecided.
