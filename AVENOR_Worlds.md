# AVENOR — Rotating World Collection

> Ten proposed large regions for the long-term collection, not a launch commitment. Names, order, dimensions and rotation cadence remain provisional.

## Shared principles
Every region is a coherent place with distinct geography, architecture and ambience. Include landmarks, quiet exploration, social locations, remote destinations, secrets, interiors, NPC activity and optional combat. Do not design an oversized arena or pad the map with empty distance. First production target is one compact district in GREYHAVEN. Measure traversal, memory, server cost and mobile frame rate before deciding full-world dimensions.

## Proposed regions
### 01 GREYHAVEN
Coastal mainland: balanced city, old harbour, suburbs, countryside, industrial quarter and remote restricted facility. Introductory all-rounder.

### 02 SOLARA
Mediterranean island: coastal town, beaches, cliffs, hills, villages, airport and remote research site. Warm coastal exploration and island geography.

### 03 NORTHPASS
Alpine region: mountain town, ski resort, pine forest, frozen lakes, tunnels, hydroelectric infrastructure and observatory. Vertical exploration and weather.

### 04 IRONVALE
Industrial basin: steelworks, freight rail, worker town, power complex, old factory quarter and river corridor. Layered interiors and infrastructure exploration.

### 05 REDSTONE
Arid inland region: roadside town, canyonlands, mining operation, desert highway, old airfield and remote compound. Rock formations and long horizons.

### 06 WESTBRIDGE
Dense metropolis: downtown, suburbs, riverfront, transit network, shopping district and construction zone. Vertical urban exploration.

### 07 PINEFALL
Forest/lake region: village, national forest, cabins, logging quarter, lake district and dam/waterworks. Quiet wilderness and remote discoveries.

### 08 EASTPORT
Maritime logistics region: port city, container terminals, shipyard, cargo airport, coastal suburbs and possible offshore facility. Waterfront industry.

### 09 DUSTFORD
Rural agricultural region: town, farms, barns, silos, highway services, railway, woodland hills and forgotten settlement. Small communities and environmental storytelling.

### 10 BLACKRIDGE
Highland plateau: mountain village, quarry, winding roads, dam valley, observatory ridge and old resort. Elevation and remote infrastructure.

## Differentiation
Greyhaven = balanced coastal mainland; Solara = island; Northpass = alpine; Ironvale = industrial production; Redstone = desert/canyon; Westbridge = dense city; Pinefall = forest/lakes; Eastport = maritime logistics; Dustford = rural agriculture; Blackridge = highland/quarry/dam.

## Per-world design package
Top-down region plan; landmarks/routes; exploration loops; interior list; NPC/faction routes; ambient activities; discoveries; safe/social and encounter spaces; lighting/weather/audio; performance budget; greybox test.

## Scope discipline
Ten regions are a long-term vision. Build one polished district, expand its region, then prove a second distinct region before scaling. Exact studs, kilometres, player capacity and full-world area remain TBD.
