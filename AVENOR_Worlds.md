# AVENOR — Rotating World Collection

> **Status: ten proposed world concepts.** The user requested at least six to ten or more maps. Ten concepts are documented as the initial target for planning. Names, order, dimensions, exact scale, player capacity and launch selection are proposals, not locked approvals. Every world is a large coherent region, not a small competitive arena.

## 1. Shared world standard

Every AVENOR world should:
- Feel like a believable modern geographical region.
- Be large enough for exploration and travel, but not padded with empty distance.
- Contain a major urban or settlement area plus several distinct subregions.
- Have memorable landmarks visible from a distance.
- Include interiors and vertical spaces worth exploring.
- Include NPC routes, activities and secrets.
- Offer natural places for player encounters without forcing all areas into combat.
- Contain a set of permanent weapon discoveries, cosmetic finds, journal entries and world activities.
- Have unique ambience, weather, lighting and soundscape.
- Support streaming and performance constraints on Roblox, including mobile.
- Be understandable through a map, signs, landmarks and discovery journal without revealing every secret.

Each world needs a safe/neutral hub proposal, travel routes, activity locations, NPC population plan, secret locations and a performance budget before production.

## 2. Proposed ten-world collection

### WORLD 01 — GREYHAVEN
**Identity:** A broad coastal mainland region with a modern city, old harbour, suburbs and surrounding countryside.

**Major areas**
- Greyhaven City: commercial centre, residential districts, rooftops and underground service spaces.
- Old Harbour: marina, warehouses, waterfront market and cargo piers.
- Outer Towns: smaller settlements, garages, shops and roadside services.
- Greenbelt: forest trails, lakes, farms and country roads.
- Industrial Quarter: factories, rail sidings and freight yards.
- Remote restricted site: a large abandoned facility with discoverable interior sections.

**World character:** The most balanced introduction to AVENOR. It demonstrates city, town, industry, coast and countryside in one region.

**Discovery ideas:** Hidden service tunnels, a sealed research wing, remote cabins, rooftop access routes and an NPC expedition that moves between districts.

### WORLD 02 — SOLARA
**Identity:** A Mediterranean-style island with a coastal town, hills, beaches and remote facilities.

**Major areas**
- Solara Town: compact city centre, residential streets and waterfront.
- Marina Coast: beaches, cliffs, piers and small harbours.
- Inland Hills: winding roads, villages, farmland and viewpoints.
- Island Airport: terminals, hangars and service buildings.
- Remote Research Site: isolated buildings away from the main settlement.
- Coastal caves and service tunnels.

**World character:** Warm light, open horizons, narrow roads and a strong sense of island geography.

**Discovery ideas:** A hidden cave route, a closed-off research wing, a remote weather station and a travelling adventurer group.

### WORLD 03 — NORTHPASS
**Identity:** A cold alpine region with a mountain town, ski resort, forests and infrastructure.

**Major areas**
- Northpass Town: shops, apartments, a small civic centre and transport depot.
- Alpine Resort: lifts, lodges, slopes and maintenance buildings.
- Pine Forest: cabins, trails, ranger facilities and frozen lakes.
- Mountain Infrastructure: tunnels, hydroelectric station and service roads.
- High Observatory: remote scientific buildings and antenna structures.
- Mountain villages and lookout points.

**World character:** Snow, mountain silhouettes, restricted visibility in selected weather, and vertical exploration.

**Discovery ideas:** A closed mountain tunnel, an observatory archive, a maintenance route behind the dam and a remote cabin with a unique NPC.

### WORLD 04 — IRONVALE
**Identity:** A dense industrial basin shaped by factories, freight rail, a worker town and power infrastructure.

**Major areas**
- Ironvale Works: steelworks, processing buildings and large yards.
- Freight District: rail lines, depots, warehouses and loading zones.
- Worker Town: homes, shops, small civic buildings and garages.
- Power Complex: substations and a large generation facility.
- Old Factory Quarter: abandoned production buildings and overgrown service roads.
- River Corridor: bridges, embankments and maintenance paths.

**World character:** Industrial scale, heavy machinery, rail corridors and layered interiors.

**Discovery ideas:** An unused factory wing, a maintenance tunnel network, an old rail control room and a hidden records archive.

### WORLD 05 — REDSTONE
**Identity:** An arid inland region with desert roads, canyon country, a mining town and isolated infrastructure.

**Major areas**
- Redstone Town: roadside businesses, homes, garages and a small commercial strip.
- Canyonlands: cliffs, ravines, trails and remote shelters.
- Mining Operation: quarry, processing plant and worker buildings.
- Desert Highway: service stations, motels and long-distance routes.
- Old Airfield: hangars, runway and disused support buildings.
- Remote research compound.

**World character:** Long sightlines, dramatic rock formations, heat haze and sparse settlements.

**Discovery ideas:** An abandoned mine level, a concealed canyon shelter, an old airfield archive and a sealed research section.

### WORLD 06 — WESTBRIDGE
**Identity:** A dense metropolitan region with downtown towers, suburbs, riverfront and public infrastructure.

**Major areas**
- Downtown: offices, shops, apartments and rooftop routes.
- Residential Suburbs: houses, parks, schools and local shops.
- Riverfront: bridges, promenades and service roads.
- Transit Network: stations, maintenance areas and underground pedestrian routes.
- Commercial District: shopping centre, hotels and parking structures.
- Construction Zone: unfinished buildings and temporary access routes.

**World character:** Vertical urban exploration, dense streets and many interior spaces.

**Discovery ideas:** A hidden transit maintenance room, an unfinished tower floor, a sealed archive and a multi-step city scavenger discovery.

### WORLD 07 — PINEFALL
**Identity:** A vast forest-and-lake region with small settlements, park facilities and remote cabins.

**Major areas**
- Pinefall Village: small town, shops, garages and local services.
- National Forest: trails, clearings, ranger posts and lookout towers.
- Lake District: docks, cabins, islands and shoreline paths.
- Logging Quarter: old mill, storage yards and forest roads.
- Remote Cabins: scattered homes and abandoned shelters.
- Dam and Waterworks: infrastructure with interior spaces.

**World character:** Quiet expanses, dense tree cover, lake reflections and remote discoveries.

**Discovery ideas:** A settlement absent from the map, an old ranger archive, a blocked forest road and an adventurer camp that moves between sites.

### WORLD 08 — EASTPORT
**Identity:** A major maritime logistics region with a port city, container terminals, shipyards and offshore infrastructure.

**Major areas**
- Eastport City: waterfront centre, residential blocks and commercial streets.
- Container Port: terminals, cranes, warehouses and service roads.
- Shipyard: dry docks, workshops and maintenance buildings.
- Cargo Airport: freight terminals and hangars.
- Coastal Suburbs: beaches, marinas and smaller neighbourhoods.
- Offshore Facility: a remote industrial installation reached by an approved transport route if vehicles/boats are implemented.

**World character:** Large industrial waterfront, cargo movement and layered port infrastructure.

**Discovery ideas:** An inactive shipyard section, a sealed cargo archive, a remote offshore room and a port worker NPC story.

### WORLD 09 — DUSTFORD
**Identity:** A rural agricultural region with farms, small towns, motels, railways and a forgotten settlement.

**Major areas**
- Dustford Town: main street, homes, shops and services.
- Farm Belt: fields, barns, silos and country lanes.
- Highway Strip: gas stations, motels, diners and repair garages.
- Railway Corridor: small stations, sidings and maintenance buildings.
- Woodland Hills: scattered cabins and trails.
- Forgotten Town: abandoned streets and hidden interiors.

**World character:** Open rural distances, small communities and environmental storytelling.

**Discovery ideas:** A locked station office, a forgotten town record room, an unusual trader route and an abandoned farm complex.

### WORLD 10 — BLACKRIDGE
**Identity:** A rugged highland plateau with steep valleys, mountain villages, a quarry and major infrastructure.

**Major areas**
- Blackridge Village: compact settlement, local shops and garages.
- Highland Roads: winding routes, bridges and tunnels.
- Quarry District: extraction site, processing buildings and service yards.
- Dam Valley: large dam, power station and river paths.
- Observatory Ridge: remote observatory and support buildings.
- Old Mountain Resort: closed lodges and maintenance routes.

**World character:** Strong elevation changes, long valley views, exposed ridges and remote infrastructure.

**Discovery ideas:** An observatory archive, a sealed dam maintenance area, a hidden route through the quarry and a remote settlement contact.

## 3. World differentiation matrix

| World | Main identity | Key secondary identity |
|---|---|---|
| Greyhaven | Balanced coastal mainland | City + countryside |
| Solara | Mediterranean island | Hills + coast |
| Northpass | Alpine winter region | Resort + infrastructure |
| Ironvale | Industrial basin | Freight + factories |
| Redstone | Arid inland region | Canyon + mining |
| Westbridge | Dense metropolis | Transit + vertical city |
| Pinefall | Forest and lakes | Wilderness + small settlements |
| Eastport | Maritime logistics | Port + shipyard |
| Dustford | Rural agricultural region | Farms + forgotten town |
| Blackridge | Highland plateau | Quarry + dam |

Avoid overlap: Greyhaven is the balanced first region; Eastport is specifically a port/logistics world; Westbridge is the dense urban world; Ironvale is the industrial production world.

## 4. Required design package for each world

Before a world is approved for production, document:
1. A top-down region map with named subregions.
2. A travel-time and traversal study.
3. Major landmarks and skyline silhouettes.
4. A list of enterable interiors.
5. NPC populations and route plans.
6. Activities and world-event hooks.
7. Weapon discovery locations and validation conditions.
8. Secrets and journal entries.
9. Safe/neutral locations and PvP rules.
10. Streaming, memory and mobile performance budget.
11. Weather, lighting and audio identity.
12. A playable greybox test with actual movement.

## 5. Scale and production status

- Ten is the proposed planning collection, not a promise that all ten ship at launch.
- Exact map dimensions and stud footprints are **TBD**. Do not invent a square-kilometre claim or final player capacity before a Roblox streaming/performance prototype.
- The user has not yet selected a final map size. Prototype one district first, measure travel time and server performance, then scale.
- Each world must be valuable on its own. Do not make ten unfinished maps to hit a number.
