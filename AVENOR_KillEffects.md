> **Current AVENOR integration rules (2026 direction):** Elimination effects remain cosmetic-only and persist permanently once owned. They may be earned through progression, discoveries, events, mastery or competitive rewards. They must remain readable in both Open World encounters and competitive modes, never hide nearby players or critical information, and must support reduced-effects settings. World rotation never removes them. The existing rarity hierarchy remains unchanged.

# AVENOR — Kill Effect Collection

> 60 individual elimination effects, ranked across the same six rarity tiers as weapon skins. Kill effects trigger only after an elimination and are cosmetic presentation, never gameplay advantages.

## Global presentation rules

- Effects play at the eliminated player's position and resolve quickly enough to keep the match moving.
- Every effect must preserve player readability, objective visibility and awareness of nearby opponents. No full-screen flashes, long camera locks, heavy screen shake, lingering fog or effects that hide other players.
- No gore or realistic injury presentation. The visual language is stylized, clean and competitive.
- Effects cannot alter damage, hitboxes, collision, movement, respawn timing, audio awareness or any gameplay information.
- Rarity increases spectacle, animation layers, sound design and transformation. It does not increase duration or gameplay impact.
- Each effect has its own identity. Effects are not weapon-specific and can be equipped independently.

## Implementation notes

- Equip one kill effect at a time. The selected effect is shown to all players so its identity remains consistent in the match.
- Effects should use a shared timing budget and a common cleanup system. Higher rarity adds visual layers, not extra lingering time.
- Keep particles, light intensity and sound volume within competitive limits. Provide settings to reduce or disable cosmetic effects without removing gameplay-critical hit or elimination feedback.
- Singular effects may have unique audio and animation assets, but must still obey the same visibility and performance limits.
