# Umbra Deep

**World ID:** WORLD_008  
**Status:** PROPOSAL

**Identity:** A vast subterranean world with enormous caverns, vertical rock formations and independent underground ecosystems.

**Visual palette:** Deep navy, charcoal, muted teal, mineral violet and carefully placed bioluminescent light.

**Major biomes**
1. **Lantern Hollows** — caverns with naturally glowing organisms.
2. **The Vertical Archive** — enormous shafts, ledges and layered stone walls.
3. **Blackwater Galleries** — underground lakes and submerged passages.

**Sub-biomes**
- **The Root Cathedral:** a cavern crossed by giant roots descending from the surface.
- **The Crystal Lung:** a chamber where mineral formations create slow, visible air currents.
- **The Blind Garden:** a hidden ecosystem with almost no natural light.

**Signature plants**
- Glowcap — Unusual.
- Stoneheart Moss — Ancient.

**Exploration identity:** Darkness, vertical navigation, cave mapping, sound, biological light and hidden underground ecosystems.

## Map production requirements
- Build a readable greybox for each major biome before final art.
- Give each biome a landmark, a main route, an optional route and a hidden discovery route.
- Place the five native species in distinct habitat anchors rather than scattering them randomly.
- Define local streaming regions and test fast traversal across each boundary.
- Keep gameplay-relevant plant clues visible at low graphics quality.
