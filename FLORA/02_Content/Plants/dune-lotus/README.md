# Dune Lotus

**Plant ID:** PLANT_39  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Unusual  
**Home world:** [Sereglass](../../../01_Worlds/sereglass/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Hidden Watercourses  
**Ability set:** Oasis Bloom

## Species identity
A pale lotus grows in shallow sand basins, opening only when underground water rises.

**Ecological role:** Water discovery and oasis creation; the plant helps players understand the hidden water cycle of its biome.

## Discovery and research
Trace a line of unusually cool sand to a concealed basin.
1. Read the local terrain and temperature clues.
2. Observe the plant's response to water conditions.
3. Investigate the hidden basin or watercourse.
4. Record the discovery in the archive.
5. Research the full ability set.
