# Coralweave

**Plant ID:** PLANT_30  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Unusual  
**Home world:** [Pelagora](../../../01_Worlds/pelagora/README.md)  
**Habitat:** Ribbon Reef  
**Ability set:** Reefcraft

## Species identity
Flexible coral branches weave into arches and soft tunnels that sway with the tide.

**Ecological role:** Water routes and flexible structures; its growth changes the flow and navigability of the surrounding reef.

## Discovery and research
Find the reef section that changes shape with the current.
1. Locate the species' unique environmental clue.
2. Observe its response to changing water movement.
3. Complete a field investigation in the native biome.
4. Record the verified discovery.
5. Research the full ability set.
