# Crystalroot

**Plant ID:** PLANT_43  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Ancient  
**Home world:** [Umbra Deep](../../../01_Worlds/umbra-deep/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Singing Geodes  
**Ability set:** Geodeheart

## Species identity
Pale roots grow through crystal seams and produce faint harmonic vibrations.

**Ecological role:** Resonance and mineral structures; it forms part of the cave's living navigation network.

## Discovery and research
Locate the root that resonates with a nearby crystal wall.
1. Observe spore or resonance patterns.
2. Locate the plant's environmental signal.
3. Investigate a connected chamber or mineral structure.
4. Record the specimen and verified clues.
5. Research its complete ability set.
