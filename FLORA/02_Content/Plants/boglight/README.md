# Boglight

**Plant ID:** PLANT_36  
**Status:** PROPOSAL  
**Rarity:** Unusual  
**Home world:** [Nacre Hollow](../../../01_Worlds/nacre-hollow/README.md)  
**Habitat:** The Lantern Fen  
**Ability set:** Mireglow

## Species identity
Small amber bulbs hover above dark wetland leaves and brighten when mist thickens.

**Ecological role:** Wetland navigation and hidden signals; its presence changes how players read the wetland and its shifting visibility.

## Discovery and research
Follow the dimmest glow to find the plant that reacts to footsteps.
1. Observe the wetland during different light conditions.
2. Identify the species' unique signal.
3. Follow a habitat-specific field clue.
4. Record the verified discovery.
5. Research the complete ability set.
