# CRE-004 — Rootmaw

- **Status:** PROPOSAL
- **Native world:** Myrrwood
- **Creature type:** Heavy, powerful quadruped
- **Signature ability:** Root Surge

## Visual identity

Rootmaw is a large, heavy quadruped with thick bark-like armor. Its powerful legs resemble twisted tree roots and end in sturdy wooden claws. Layered wooden plates cover its back, with moss and tiny glowing plants between them. Its head has a distinctive flower-shaped jaw made from wooden petal structures and deep amber eyes.

## Distinctive identification cues

- Heavy quadrupedal body.
- Bark armor and layered wooden plates.
- Thick root-like limbs and wooden claws.
- Flower-shaped jaw or petal-like head structures.
- Moss and small plants growing between armor plates.

Do not confuse it with Mossback: Rootmaw is rigid, bark-armored and root-limbed; Mossback is rounded and covered in soft moss.

## Behaviour and ability

Rootmaw can remain still and blend into the forest floor. **Root Surge:** it strikes the ground, causing thick twisting roots to emerge and spread outward. Small plants and glowing particles appear between the roots.

## Habitat cues

Myrrwood ancient forest, giant exposed roots, hanging vines and dense vegetation.
