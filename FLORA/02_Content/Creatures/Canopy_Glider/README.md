# CRE-003 — Canopy Glider

- **Status:** PROPOSAL
- **Native world:** Myrrwood
- **Creature type:** Aerial gliding forest creature
- **Signature ability:** Leafwind Glide

## Visual identity

Canopy Glider is a lightweight, medium-sized creature with a streamlined body, four limbs and two enormous leaf-like gliding membranes extending from its sides. The membranes have visible leaf veins and green-to-turquoise gradients. It has a long balancing tail, expressive eyes and small branch-like structures along its head.

## Distinctive identification cues

- Two large leaf-shaped membranes extending from the sides.
- Visible botanical veins across the membranes.
- Lightweight body and long balancing tail.
- Green and turquoise palette.
- Usually shown gliding between elevated trees.

## Behaviour and ability

It glides between high branches and rests in the forest canopy. **Leafwind Glide:** swirling green leaves and wind particles form a current beneath the creature, carrying it through the air and leaving curved leaf trails.

## Habitat cues

Myrrwood's enormous trees, elevated branches, hanging vines, giant roots and shafts of sunlight through the canopy.
