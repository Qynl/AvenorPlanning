# Scanning and Research

Scanning begins an investigation. It should not instantly identify every species or complete a discovery.

## Proposed investigation stages
1. **Notice:** player finds an unfamiliar plant or ecological clue.
2. **Scan:** scanner records visible traits and environmental context.
3. **Investigate:** player observes behaviour, habitat or reactions.
4. **Experiment:** player performs a suitable field interaction or returns under a different condition.
5. **Record:** discovery is added to the permanent research archive.
6. **Research:** milestones unlock abilities from the plant's set.
7. **Master:** additional understanding, mastery rewards or special interactions.

## Requirements
- Research should teach players how species behave.
- Clues should be readable and logically connected to the environment.
- Important progress persists across world rotation.
- Ability unlock milestones are visible in the plant document and ability set.
- Routine scans remain quick; major discoveries can receive cinematic presentation.

Exact scanner interface, research currency, experiment rules and mastery rewards remain TBD.
