# Progression System

## Progression layers
- **Discovery archive:** permanent record of identified species and field observations.
- **Research:** progress through plant-specific milestones.
- **Ability unlocks:** abilities are unlocked within their parent plant set.
- **Mastery:** continued use and understanding may unlock mastery rewards.
- **World knowledge:** clues and discoveries can open routes to other regions or worlds.

## Principles
- Progress persists through world rotation.
- Do not make rare plants mandatory for basic progression.
- Reward both exploration and encounter mastery.
- Avoid turning discovery into repetitive chores.
- Keep exact economy, XP, levels, currencies and reward pacing TBD until the full loop is tested.
