# AVENOR — Individual Weapon Skin Collection

> **Roster status:** Cosmetics for the 40 active firearms remain active launch candidates. Cosmetics attached to archived firearm concepts are retained below for reference but are **archive-only**, not launch unlocks. Utility and melee cosmetics remain active unless their weapon entry says otherwise.

> Two individually designed skins per weapon. Every skin is exclusive to its named weapon. No universal patterns or cross-weapon compatibility. Rarity controls cosmetic complexity only; no gameplay advantages.

## Rarity presentation standards

- **Commonplace:** clean recolours, restrained patterns, subtle material changes; no major animated effects.
- **Altered:** distinctive materials, custom finish details and small visual accents.
- **Evolved:** animated details, emissive elements and restrained custom trails or action effects.
- **Ascendant:** elaborate animation, layered visual effects and bespoke firing or action presentation.
- **Extraordinary:** major visual transformation, elaborate effects and custom sound presentation.
- **Singular:** signature-level transformation, exceptional animation and a complete, highly distinctive visual identity.

All effects must preserve weapon silhouette, aim clarity, projectile readability, hit feedback and competitive visibility. Effects are cosmetic and cannot change damage, accuracy, recoil, hitboxes, tracking or concealment.
